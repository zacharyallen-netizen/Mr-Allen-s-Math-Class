Unit 7 Guided Notes — Circles, Area, Surface Area and Volume

Find the circumference and area of circles, and the area, surface area and volume of shapes and prisms.

  • I can find the area of a circle and solve problems with circles

1 Circumference NC.7.G.4

📖 Words to know
Radius: center to edge.
Diameter: edge to edge through the center. d = 2r.
Circumference: the distance around.
π: about 3.14. For EVERY circle, C ÷ d = π.
📌 THE BIG IDEA: C = πd or C = 2πr. The distance around is a little more than 3 diameters. Use 3.14 for π unless the problem says to leave π.
r = 5 cm, so d = 10 cm and C = 3.14 × 10 = 31.4 cm.

381.64 inches
⚠ Watch out: C = 2πr uses the RADIUS. If you are given the diameter, use C = πd (or cut it in half first).
Find the circumference of a circle with diameter 10 cm. Use 3.14.
3.14 × 10 = 31.4 cm.
A circle has radius 3 m. What is its circumference? Use 3.14.
C = 2πr = 2 × 3.14 × 3 = 18.84 m.
A round table has a circumference of 43.96 ft. What is its diameter, in feet? Use 3.14.
43.96 ÷ 3.14 = 14 ft.

2 Area of a Circle NC.7.G.4

📖 Words to know
Area: the space inside, in square units.
r²: r × r, not r × 2.
📌 THE BIG IDEA: A = πr². Cut a circle into thin slices and line them up: it makes a shape like a rectangle, πr long and r tall. So the area is πr × r = πr².
d = 10 in, so r = 5 in and A = 3.14 × 5 × 5 = 78.5 in².

r = 5: A = 3.14 × ? = ?

r = 10: A = ?

d = 6: r = ?, A = 3.14 × 9 = ?

Example: a half circle has diameter 8 m. What is its area?

1r = 4
2Whole circle: 3.14 × 4 × 4 = 50.24
3Half: 50.24 ÷ 2
425.12 m²
⚠ Watch out: Square the RADIUS, not the diameter, and do not multiply r by 2. r² means r × r.
Find the area of a circle with radius 10 ft. Use 3.14.
3.14 × 100 = 314 ft².
A pizza has a diameter of 12 in. What is its area? Use 3.14.
r = 6. A = 3.14 × 36 = 113.04 in².
A sprinkler waters a circle 10 m across. It only turns through 1/4 of the circle. What area does it water, in m²? Use 3.14.
3.14 × 25 = 78.5. 78.5 ÷ 4 = 19.625 m².

Composite shape: a shape made of rectangles, triangles and other simple shapes.
Trapezoid: A = ½(b₁ + b₂)h
📌 THE BIG IDEA: Break the shape into pieces you know. Find each area, then ADD them. Or find a big rectangle and SUBTRACT the missing piece.

A rectangle is 10 ft by 6 ft. A 4 ft by 3 ft rectangle is cut out of it. What area is left, in ft²?
10 × 6 = 60. 4 × 3 = 12. 60 − 12 = 48 ft².

4 Surface Area of Prisms NC.7.G.6

📖 Words to know
Face: a flat side of a 3-D shape.
Net: a 3-D shape unfolded flat.
Surface area: the sum of the areas of ALL the faces.
📌 THE BIG IDEA: Unfold the prism into a net. Find the area of every face and add. A rectangular prism has 3 pairs of matching faces: SA = 2(lw + lh + wh).
5 × 3 front and back, 5 × 2 top and bottom, 2 × 3 left and right.

Front and back: 2 × (5 × 3) = ?

Top and bottom: 2 × (5 × 2) = ?

Left and right: 2 × (2 × 3) = ?

Surface area = ? cm²

Example: a triangular prism. The triangle has legs 6 and 8 and long side 10. The prism is 12 long.

1Two triangles: 2 × ½ × 6 × 8 = 48
2Three rectangles: 6 × 12 + 8 × 12 + 10 × 12 = 288
348 + 288 = 336 square units
⚠ Watch out: Count EVERY face. A box has 6 faces, not 3. Surface area is in square units.
A cube has edges of 3 in. What is its surface area, in in²?
6 × 9 = 54 in².
A box is 6 cm long, 4 cm wide and 3 cm tall. What is its surface area?
2(24 + 18 + 12) = 2 × 54 = 108 cm².
A square box with no lid has a 4 ft by 4 ft bottom and is 10 ft tall. How many ft² of wood does it take?
Bottom 16 + sides 4 × 40 = 160. 16 + 160 = 176 ft².

5 Volume of Prisms NC.7.G.6

📖 Words to know
Base (B): the area of the shape on the end of the prism.
Volume: how many unit cubes fit inside; cubic units.
📌 THE BIG IDEA: V = Bh: area of the base × height (the length of the prism). For a box, B = lw, so V = lwh. For a triangular prism, B = ½bh of the triangle.
B = 5 × 3 = 15. V = 15 × 2 = 30 ft³.

Box 5 by 3 by 2: V = ?

Triangular prism: triangle ½ × 6 × 4 = ?, length 10: V = ?

Cube with 4 in edges: V = ? in³

Example: a fish tank is 12 in long, 10 in wide and 8 in tall. Water fills it to 5 in. How much MORE water fits?

1Whole tank: 12 × 10 × 8 = 960
2Water now: 12 × 10 × 5 = 600
3360 in³ more
⚠ Watch out: Volume is in CUBIC units (in³). Area is in square units. Do not mix them up.
A box is 5 cm by 3 cm by 2 cm. What is its volume, in cm³?
5 × 3 × 2 = 30 cm³.
A triangular prism has a triangle base with b = 6 and h = 4, and the prism is 10 long. What is the volume?
B = 12. V = 12 × 10 = 120 cubic units.
A box holds 240 in³. Its base is 8 in by 5 in. How tall is it, in inches?
8 × 5 = 40. 240 ÷ 40 = 6 in.
