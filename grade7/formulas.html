Shapes: area

Rectangle

A = bh

A 6 by 4 rectangle: A = 6 × 4 = 24 square units.

Parallelogram

A = bh

Use the height that makes a square corner with the base, not the slanted side.

Triangle

A = ½bh

Half of a rectangle. b = 10, h = 6: A = ½ × 10 × 6 = 30.

Trapezoid

A = ½h(b₁ + b₂)

Add the two bases, multiply by the height, then take half.

Circles

Circumference

C = πd = 2πr

The distance around. d = 10: C = 10π ≈ 31.4.

Area of a circle

A = πr²

r = 3: A = π × 3² = 9π ≈ 28.26. Square the radius first.

Radius and diameter

d = 2r · r = d ÷ 2

Use π ≈ 3.14, or leave the answer in terms of π.

Solids: volume and surface area

Volume of a prism

V = Bh

B is the AREA of the base (not a side length). Box 5 × 3 × 4: B = 15, V = 15 × 4 = 60 cubic units.

Surface area

SA = sum of the areas of all the faces

Unfold it into a net. A box has 3 pairs of matching faces: SA = 2lw + 2lh + 2wh.

Angles

Complementary

a + b = 90°

Two angles that make a right angle.

Supplementary

a + b = 180°

Two angles that make a straight line.

Vertical angles

a = b

Angles across from each other where two lines cross are equal.

Triangle

a + b + c = 180°

The three angles of any triangle add to 180°.

Ratios and percents

Unit rate

unit rate = amount ÷ 1 unit

$6 for 4 pounds: 6 ÷ 4 = $1.50 per pound.

Proportional

y = kx

k is the constant of proportionality (the unit rate): k = y ÷ x.

Percent of a number

part = percent × whole

30% of 60 = 0.30 × 60 = 18.

Discount

sale price = (1 − rate) × price

20% off $50: 0.80 × 50 = $40.

Tax or tip

total = (1 + rate) × price

6% tax on $30: 1.06 × 30 = $31.80.

Simple interest

I = Prt

$200 at 5% for 3 years: 200 × 0.05 × 3 = $30.

Percent change

percent change = change ÷ original

$40 up to $50: 10 ÷ 40 = 0.25 = 25% increase.

Statistics and probability

Mean

mean = sum ÷ count

4, 6, 8: 18 ÷ 3 = 6.

Median

the middle number in order

Put the numbers in order first.

Range

range = biggest − smallest

Shows how spread out the data is.

Probability

P(event) = good outcomes ÷ all outcomes

Always from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain).

Not happening

P(not A) = 1 − P(A)

P(win) = 0.3, so P(not win) = 0.7.

Counting outcomes

choices × choices

A coin and a number cube: 2 × 6 = 12 outcomes.
