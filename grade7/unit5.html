Unit 5 — Percents

Find a percent of a number, the whole, or the percent; solve multi-step percent problems with tax, tips, discounts, markups, commission, interest and percent error.

Standards: NC.7.RP.3 · NC.7.EE.3

U1 U2 U3 U4 U5 U6 U7 U8 U9

What you will learn in Unit 5

Learning targets

  • I can find a percent of a number, and find the whole or the percent when I know the other two
  • I can find the percent increase or percent decrease
  • I can find a total with tax, tip, fees, markups, discounts and commission
  • I can find simple interest with I = Prt
  • I can find the percent error of an estimate or a measurement

Standards

  • 7.RP.3Use proportional relationships to solve multi-step ratio and percent problems: interest, tax, markups and markdowns, gratuities and commissions, fees, percent increase and decrease, percent error.
  • 7.EE.3Solve multi-step real-world problems with rational numbers; convert between forms and check that answers make sense.
