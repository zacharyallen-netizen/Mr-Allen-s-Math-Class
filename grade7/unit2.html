Unit 2 — Expressions

Combine like terms, use the distributive property, factor out a GCF, and rewrite expressions to see what they mean.

Standards: NC.7.EE.1 · NC.7.EE.2

U1 U2 U3 U4 U5 U6 U7 U8 U9

What you will learn in Unit 2

Learning targets

  • I can name the terms, coefficients, constants and like terms in an expression
  • I can combine like terms, even with fractions, decimals and negatives
  • I can use the distributive property to expand an expression
  • I can factor an expression by taking out the greatest common factor (GCF)
  • I can add and subtract expressions, and rewrite an expression to show what it means

Standards

  • 7.EE.1Add, subtract, expand and factor linear expressions with rational coefficients.
  • 7.EE.2Rewrite an expression in a different form to see how the quantities are related.
