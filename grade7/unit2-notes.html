Unit 2 Guided Notes — Expressions

Combine like terms, distribute, factor, and write expressions that tell a story.

By the end of this unit, I can…
  • I can name the terms, coefficients, constants and like terms in an expression
  • I can combine like terms, even with fractions, decimals and negatives
  • I can use the distributive property to expand an expression
  • I can factor an expression by taking out the greatest common factor (GCF)
  • I can add and subtract expressions, and rewrite an expression to show what it means

1 Terms and Like Terms NC.7.EE.1

📖 Words to know
Term: a part of an expression joined by + or −. 4x − 7 + y has 3 terms.
Coefficient: the number in front of a variable. In −3x, it is −3.
Constant: a number with no variable. In 5x + 8, it is 8.
Like terms: terms with the same variable part: 2x and −5x, or 6 and −1.
📌 THE BIG IDEA: To simplify, combine like terms: add or subtract the coefficients. The variable part stays the same. Keep each sign with the term after it.
ExpressionTermsCoefficient of xConstant
5x − 35x, −3??
−x + 12−x, 12??
0.4x + 2y − 60.4x, 2y, −6??

Example: simplify 7x + 4 − 3x − 9

1Find like terms: 7x and −3x. 4 and −9.
2Combine the x-terms: 7x − 3x = 4x
3Combine the constants: 4 − 9 = −5
44x − 5

Example: simplify 12x + 3 − 34x

1x-terms: 12x − 34x = 24x − 34x
2= −14x
3−14x + 3
⚠ Watch out: x and x² are NOT like terms. And x means 1x, so 5x + x = 6x, not 5x.
Simplify: 6y + 2 − 4y + 5. What is the coefficient of y in your answer?
6y − 4y = 2y. 2 + 5 = 7. The answer is 2y + 7, so the coefficient is 2.
Simplify: −2.5m + 4 + 1.5m − 7
−2.5m + 1.5m = −1m = −m. 4 − 7 = −3. Answer: −m − 3.
Jo says 3x + 4y simplifies to 7xy. Which is true?
3x and 4y have different variables, so they are not like terms. 3x + 4y is already simplified.

2 The Distributive Property NC.7.EE.1

📖 Words to know
Distributive property: a(b + c) = ab + ac. Multiply the outside number by EVERY term inside.
Expand: rewrite a product as a sum, like 3(x + 2) = 3x + 6.
📌 THE BIG IDEA: Multiply the number outside the parentheses by each term inside. Watch the signs: a negative outside changes the sign of every term inside.
3 groups of (x + 2) = 3 x's and 3 twos = 3x + 6.

4(x + 5) = 4x + ?

−2(3x − 7) = ? + ?

12(8x − 6) = ? − ?

Example: −34(8x − 12)

1Multiply −34 × 8x = −6x
2Multiply −34 × (−12) = +9
3−6x + 9

Example: 5 − 2(x − 4)

1Distribute the −2 (the sign goes with it): −2 × x = −2x and −2 × (−4) = +8
25 − 2x + 8
3Combine constants: −2x + 13
⚠ Watch out: Multiply EVERY term inside. 3(x + 4) is 3x + 12, not 3x + 4. And in 5 − 2(x − 4), the 2 is negative.
Expand: 6(2x + 3). What is the constant term?
6(2x + 3) = 12x + 18. The constant is 18.
Expand: −4(2.5x − 3)
−4 × 2.5x = −10x. −4 × (−3) = 12. Answer: −10x + 12.
Which expression is equivalent to 9 − 3(2x − 1)?
−3(2x − 1) = −6x + 3. 9 − 6x + 3 = −6x + 12.

3 Factoring with the GCF NC.7.EE.1

📖 Words to know
Greatest common factor (GCF): the biggest number that divides every term.
Factor (verb): write as a product. Factoring undoes distributing.
📌 THE BIG IDEA: To factor, find the GCF of the terms. Write it outside the parentheses. Inside, write what is left after dividing each term by the GCF. Check by distributing.

12x + 18: the GCF of 12 and 18 is ?, so 12x + 18 = 6(?)

15x − 10 = ?(3x − 2)

With a negative GCF: −8x + 20 = −4(?)

Example: factor 24x − 36

1GCF of 24 and 36 is 12.
224x ÷ 12 = 2x and −36 ÷ 12 = −3
312(2x − 3). Check: 12 × 2x − 12 × 3 = 24x − 36 ✓
⚠ Watch out: Use the GREATEST common factor. 24x − 36 = 2(12x − 18) is true, but it is not fully factored.
Factor 9x + 21 = 3(□x + 7). What number goes in the box?
9x ÷ 3 = 3x. So 9x + 21 = 3(3x + 7).
Which shows 20x − 30 factored completely?
GCF = 10. 20x ÷ 10 = 2x, −30 ÷ 10 = −3. Answer: 10(2x − 3).
Factor out −6: −6x + 9 = −3(2x − □). Which number goes in the box? (Look carefully: the GCF used is −3.)
−6x ÷ (−3) = 2x. 9 ÷ (−3) = −3. So −6x + 9 = −3(2x − 3).

4 Adding and Subtracting Expressions NC.7.EE.1

📖 Words to know
Sum of expressions: add them, then combine like terms.
Difference of expressions: subtract EVERY term of the second expression.
📌 THE BIG IDEA: To add expressions, combine like terms. To subtract an expression, distribute the minus sign to every term in the parentheses (add the opposite), then combine.

(3x + 5) + (2x − 8) = ?

(3x + 5) − (2x − 8) = 3x + 5 − 2x ? = ?

Example: (34x − 2) − (12x − 5)

1Change the signs in the second parentheses: 34x − 2 − 12x + 5
2x-terms: 34x − 24x = 14x
3Constants: −2 + 5 = 3
414x + 3
⚠ Watch out: The minus sign goes to EVERY term: −(2x − 8) = −2x + 8. Many students forget to change the second sign.
Add: (4x − 1) + (6x + 9). What is the constant in the answer?
4x + 6x = 10x. −1 + 9 = 8. Answer: 10x + 8.
Subtract: (5y + 2) − (3y − 4)
5y − 3y = 2y. 2 + 4 = 6. Answer: 2y + 6.
Find the mistake: (8x − 3) − (2x − 5) = 8x − 3 − 2x − 5 = 6x − 8. What went wrong?
−(2x − 5) = −2x + 5. 8x − 2x = 6x and −3 + 5 = 2. Answer: 6x + 2.

5 Rewriting Expressions to Show Meaning NC.7.EE.2

📖 Words to know
Equivalent expressions: expressions that are equal for every value of the variable.
📌 THE BIG IDEA: Different forms of the same expression can tell different stories. Adding 5% to a price p is p + 0.05p. Rewritten, it is 1.05p: the new price is 105% of the old price.

p + 0.05p = ?p

A 20% discount: p − 0.2p = ?p, which means you pay ?% of the price.

Perimeter of a rectangle: 2L + 2W = ?(L + W)

Example: a square garden with side s has a 2 ft path around it. The fence is 4(s + 4) ft. Write it another way.

1Distribute: 4(s + 4) = 4s + 16
24s is the garden's perimeter. 16 is the extra fence for the path.
34s + 16 shows the extra 16 ft clearly.
⚠ Watch out: Two expressions are equivalent only if they are equal for EVERY value of the variable, not just one.
A shirt costs p dollars. The tax is 7%. The total is p + 0.07p. Write it as one term: the total = □p. What goes in the box?
p + 0.07p = 1.07p.
A store marks every item down 25%. Which expression shows the sale price of an item that costs c dollars?
c − 0.25c = 0.75c. You pay 75% of the price.
Two expressions show the cost of a field trip for n students: 12n + 60 and 12(n + 5). What does the 5 in 12(n + 5) most likely mean?
12(n + 5) = 12n + 60. The 5 is 5 extra people who each pay $12, for an extra $60.

The answer is 7.

The answer is 9(2x + 3).

C. Expressions in Context

A rectangle is (x + 3) long and 4 wide. Its area is 4(x + 3). If x = 5, what is the area?
The answer is 32.
Tickets cost $8 each plus a $2 fee each. Which expression is the cost of t tickets?
8t + 2t = 10t.
A phone costs p dollars. It is on sale for 30% off, then a 6% tax is added to the sale price. Which expression is the final cost?
Sale price = 0.7p. With tax: 1.06 × 0.7p = 0.742p.
