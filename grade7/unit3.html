Unit 3 — Equations and Inequalities

Solve px + q = r and p(x + q) = r, solve and graph inequalities, and write equations and inequalities for word problems.

Standards: NC.7.EE.3 · NC.7.EE.4

U1 U2 U3 U4 U5 U6 U7 U8 U9

What you will learn in Unit 3

Learning targets

  • I can solve two-step equations like 3x + 7 = 22 and x/4 − 2 = 5
  • I can solve equations with parentheses like 4(x − 2) = 20
  • I can solve equations with fractions and decimals, and write equations for word problems
  • I can solve an inequality, flip the sign when I multiply or divide by a negative, and graph it
  • I can write and solve an inequality for a word problem and explain what the answer means

Standards

  • 7.EE.3Solve multi-step real-world problems with rational numbers in any form; check that answers make sense.
  • 7.EE.4Write and solve equations (px + q = r, p(x + q) = r) and inequalities (px + q > r, px + q < r) and graph the solutions.
