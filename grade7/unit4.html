Unit 4 — Proportional Relationships

Compute unit rates with fractions, decide if a table or graph is proportional, find the constant of proportionality, and write and use y = kx.

Standards: NC.7.RP.1 · NC.7.RP.2

U1 U2 U3 U4 U5 U6 U7 U8 U9

What you will learn in Unit 4

Learning targets

  • I can find a unit rate, even when the numbers are fractions
  • I can tell from a table if two quantities are proportional and find the constant of proportionality
  • I can tell from a graph if a relationship is proportional and explain what (1, r) and (0, 0) mean
  • I can write an equation y = kx for a proportional relationship
  • I can use a constant of proportionality to solve real-world problems

Standards

  • 7.RP.1Compute unit rates with ratios of fractions, including lengths, areas and other quantities.
  • 7.RP.2Recognize and represent proportional relationships in tables, graphs, equations and words; find the constant of proportionality; explain what a point means.
