Unit 6 — Scale Drawings, Angles and Triangles

Find actual lengths and areas from scale drawings, decide when three parts make one, many or no triangles, and write and solve equations for complementary, supplementary, vertical and adjacent angles.

Standards: NC.7.G.1 · NC.7.G.2 · NC.7.G.5

U1 U2 U3 U4 U5 U6 U7 U8 U9

What you will learn in Unit 6

Learning targets

  • I can use a scale to find actual lengths from a scale drawing
  • I can find actual areas and redraw a picture at a new scale
  • I can tell if three side lengths or three angles make one triangle, many triangles or no triangle
  • I can name complementary, supplementary, vertical and adjacent angles
  • I can write and solve an equation to find an unknown angle

Standards

  • 7.G.1Solve problems with scale drawings: actual lengths and areas, and redraw at a different scale.
  • 7.G.2Draw shapes with given conditions; focus on triangles from three angles or three sides (one, many or no triangles).
  • 7.G.5Use supplementary, complementary, vertical and adjacent angles to write and solve equations for an unknown angle.
