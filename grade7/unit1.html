Unit 1 — Operations with Rational Numbers

Add, subtract, multiply and divide positive and negative numbers, and change fractions to decimals.

Standards: NC.7.NS.1 · NC.7.NS.2 · NC.7.NS.3

U1 U2 U3 U4 U5 U6 U7 U8 U9

What you will learn in Unit 1

Learning targets

  • I can add and subtract integers and other rational numbers, and show it on a number line
  • I can find the distance between two numbers on a number line
  • I can multiply and divide positive and negative numbers
  • I can change a fraction to a decimal and tell if it ends or repeats
  • I can solve real-world problems with positive and negative numbers

Standards

  • 7.NS.1Add and subtract rational numbers; show them on a number line.
  • 7.NS.2Multiply and divide rational numbers; change a fraction to a decimal with long division.
  • 7.NS.3Solve real-world problems with the four operations on rational numbers.
