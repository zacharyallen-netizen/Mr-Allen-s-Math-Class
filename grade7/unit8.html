Unit 8 — Statistics

Tell a fair sample from a biased one, use a sample to predict about a population, find mean, median, range, IQR and MAD, and compare two groups.

Standards: NC.7.SP.1 · NC.7.SP.2 · NC.7.SP.3 · NC.7.SP.4

U1 U2 U3 U4 U5 U6 U7 U8 U9

What you will learn in Unit 8

Learning targets

  • I can tell if a sample is random and fair, or biased
  • I can use a random sample to predict something about a whole population
  • I can find the mean, median, range, IQR and MAD of a data set
  • I can compare two data sets by their centers and spreads and say how much they overlap
  • I can use samples to compare two populations and explain my answer

Standards

  • 7.SP.1Understand that statistics can be used to learn about a population from a sample; random samples are more likely to represent it.
  • 7.SP.2Use data from a random sample to draw inferences about a population; see how much samples vary.
  • 7.SP.3Judge how much two data sets overlap: compare the difference in centers to a measure of spread.
  • 7.SP.4Use measures of center and variability from random samples to compare two populations.
