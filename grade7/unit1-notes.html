1 Adding Integers and Rational Numbers NC.7.NS.1

📖 Words to know
Opposites: two numbers the same distance from 0 on different sides, like 4 and −4.
Absolute value: how far a number is from 0. |−6| = 6 and |6| = 6.
Additive inverse: a number plus its opposite makes 0. 8 + (−8) = 0.
📌 THE BIG IDEA: To add on a number line, start at the first number. Move RIGHT to add a positive. Move LEFT to add a negative.
3 + (−5): start at 3, move 5 to the left. You land on −2.

Same signs: add the absolute values and keep the sign. −4 + (−3) = ?

Different signs: subtract the absolute values. Keep the sign of the number that is farther from 0. −9 + 4 = ?

A number plus its opposite is always ?.

Example 1: −2.5 + (−1.5)

1Same signs (both negative).
2Add the absolute values: 2.5 + 1.5 = 4
3Keep the sign: −4

Example 2: 34 + (−2)

1Different signs. Write 2 as 84.
2Subtract the absolute values: 84 − 34 = 54
3−2 is farther from 0, so the answer is negative: −54 = −114
⚠ Watch out: Do not just look at the first sign. In −9 + 4, the answer takes the sign of −9 because 9 is bigger than 4.
Find the sum: −8 + (−6)
Same signs. 8 + 6 = 14. Keep the sign: −14.
Find the sum: −3.7 + 5.2
5.2 − 3.7 = 1.5. 5.2 is farther from 0 and positive, so +1.5.
The temperature was −12°F. It rose 15°F, then fell 7°F. What is the temperature now (in °F)?
−12 + 15 = 3. Then 3 + (−7) = −4. It is −4°F.

2 Subtracting Rational Numbers NC.7.NS.1

📖 Words to know
Difference: the answer to a subtraction problem.
Distance between two numbers: the absolute value of their difference: |a − b|.
📌 THE BIG IDEA: Subtracting is the same as adding the opposite. a − b = a + (−b). Keep, change, change: keep the first number, change − to +, change the second number to its opposite.
The distance between −5 and 2 is |2 − (−5)| = |7| = 7 units.

6 − (−4) = 6 + ? = ?

−3 − 5 = −3 + ? = ?

The distance between −5 and 2 is |2 − (−5)| = ?

Example: −7 − (−10)

1Keep −7. Change − to +. Change −10 to +10.
2−7 + 10
3Different signs: 10 − 7 = 3. 10 is farther from 0, so the answer is positive: 3

Example: How far apart are −4.5 and 3?

1Distance = |3 − (−4.5)|
2= |3 + 4.5| = |7.5|
3= 7.5 units
⚠ Watch out: Subtracting a negative makes the number BIGGER. 5 − (−2) = 7, not 3.
Find the difference: 4 − 11
4 + (−11) = −7.
Find the difference: −2.5 − (−6)
−2.5 + 6 = 3.5.
A diver is at −35 ft. A bird flies 20 ft above the water. How many feet apart are they?
|20 − (−35)| = |20 + 35| = 55 feet.

Product: the answer to a multiplication problem.
Quotient: the answer to a division problem.
Undefined: has no answer. Dividing by 0 is undefined.

4 Fractions to Decimals NC.7.NS.2

📖 Words to know
Terminating decimal: a decimal that ends, like 0.25.
Repeating decimal: a decimal with a digit or group of digits that repeats forever, like 0.666…
Bar notation: a bar over the digits that repeat: 0.6̅ = 0.666…
📌 THE BIG IDEA: A fraction means division: ab = a ÷ b. Use long division. If you get a remainder of 0, the decimal terminates. If a remainder repeats, the decimal repeats.

38 = 3 ÷ 8 = ? (terminates)

23 = 2 ÷ 3 = ? (repeats)

511 = ? (the block 45 repeats)

Example: write 712 as a decimal

17 ÷ 12. 12 does not go into 7, so write 7.000…
270 ÷ 12 = 5 R 10. 100 ÷ 12 = 8 R 4. 40 ÷ 12 = 3 R 4.
3The remainder 4 repeats, so the 3 repeats: 0.583̅
⚠ Watch out: The bar goes only over the digits that repeat. 0.583̅ means 0.58333…, not 0.583583…
Write 920 as a decimal.
9/20 = 45/100 = 0.45
Which is the decimal for 49?
4 ÷ 9 = 0.444… = 0.4̅
Which fraction does NOT make a terminating decimal?
5 ÷ 6 = 0.8333… The 3 repeats forever.

5 Solving Problems with Rational Numbers NC.7.NS.3

📖 Words to know
Deposit: money put in (positive).
Withdrawal: money taken out (negative).
Elevation: height above (+) or below (−) sea level.
📌 THE BIG IDEA: Decide what each number means. Gains, deposits and going up are positive. Losses, withdrawals and going down are negative. Then add, subtract, multiply or divide.

A loss of $12 each day for 5 days: 5 × (−12) = ? dollars

Going from 40 ft below sea level to 15 ft above: 15 − (−40) = ? ft

Example: A stock lost $1.25 each day for 4 days. Then it gained $3.50. What is the total change?

1Loss for 4 days: 4 × (−1.25) = −5
2Then the gain: −5 + 3.50 = −1.50
3Total change: −$1.50 (a $1.50 loss)
⚠ Watch out: Read for the question. "How far apart" means distance (always positive). "What is the change" can be negative.
Ava has $45. She spends $18.75, then earns $12. How many dollars does she have now?
45 − 18.75 = 26.25. 26.25 + 12 = 38.25. She has $38.25.
A submarine is at −240 ft. It rises 14 of that distance. What is its new elevation (in ft)?
1/4 of 240 = 60. −240 + 60 = −180 ft.
A cave is 132 ft deep (−132 ft). A climber goes down in 4 equal steps to the bottom. Mia says each step is +33 ft. What is her mistake?
−132 ÷ 4 = −33. Each step is −33 ft (33 ft down).

C. Real-World Problems

The low was −5°C and the high was 9°C. How many degrees warmer was the high?
The answer is 14.
A hiker starts at 120 m. She goes down 15.5 m each hour for 3 hours. What is her elevation (in m)?
3 × (−15.5) = −46.5. 120 − 46.5 = 73.5 m.
Leo owes his brother $24. He pays it back in equal amounts over 6 weeks. Which expression shows the change in his debt each week?
24 ÷ 6 = 4. The debt goes down $4 each week.
