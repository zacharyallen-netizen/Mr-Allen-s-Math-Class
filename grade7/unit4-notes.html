Unit 4 Guided Notes — Proportional Relationships

Find unit rates (even with fractions), spot proportional relationships, and write y = kx.

By the end of this unit, I can…

  • I can tell from a graph if a relationship is proportional and explain what (1, r) and (0, 0) mean

1 Unit Rates with Fractions NC.7.RP.1

📖 Words to know
Unit rate: how much for ONE unit: miles per 1 hour, dollars per 1 pound.
Complex fraction: a fraction with fractions inside it, like (1/2) ÷ (1/4).
📌 THE BIG IDEA: Unit rate = first amount ÷ second amount. With fractions, divide by multiplying by the reciprocal: ab ÷ cd = ab × dc.
Walk 1/2 mile in 1/4 hour. Keep going: 2 miles in 1 hour. The unit rate is 2 miles per hour.

1/2 mile in 1/4 hour: (1/2) ÷ (1/4) = (1/2) × ? = ? miles per hour

3/4 cup of flour for 1/2 batch: (3/4) ÷ (1/2) = ? cups per batch

Example: Mia paints 2/3 of a wall in 1/6 of an hour. How many walls per hour?

1Walls ÷ hours: 23 ÷ 16
2Multiply by the reciprocal: 23 × 61 = 123
34 walls per hour
⚠ Watch out: Put the units in the right order. "Miles per hour" means miles ÷ hours, not hours ÷ miles.
A car goes 150 miles in 3 hours. What is the unit rate in miles per hour?
150 ÷ 3 = 50 miles per hour.
A snail crawls 3/4 of a foot in 1/2 of a minute. What is its speed in feet per minute?
(3/4) ÷ (1/2) = (3/4) × 2 = 6/4 = 1 1/2 feet per minute.
Ty mows 5/2 acres in 5/6 of an hour. Lu mows 2 acres in 3/4 of an hour. How many acres per hour does the FASTER mower cut?
Ty: (5/2) ÷ (5/6) = (5/2) × (6/5) = 3. Lu: 2 ÷ (3/4) = 8/3 ≈ 2.67. Ty is faster: 3 acres per hour.

3 Proportional Graphs NC.7.RP.2

📖 Words to know
Origin: the point (0, 0).
(1, r): the point where x = 1. Its y-value r is the unit rate.
📌 THE BIG IDEA: A proportional graph is a straight line through the origin. The point (1, r) shows the unit rate. The point (0, 0) means 0 of one thing goes with 0 of the other.
Straight line through (0, 0). The point (1, 3) means 3 miles in 1 hour: k = 3.

2She earns $40 for 5 hours of work.
3Unit rate: 40 ÷ 5 = 8, so the point (1, 8) is also on the line.
⚠ Watch out: A straight line that does NOT go through (0, 0) is not proportional. A curve is not proportional.
The graph shows cost (y) for pounds of grapes (x). The line passes through (0, 0) and (4, 12). What is the cost for 1 pound in dollars?
12 ÷ 4 = 3. The point (1, 3) is on the line: $3 per pound.
Which graph shows a proportional relationship?

A proportional graph of minutes (x) and pages read (y) goes through (6, 15). Which point is also on the line, and what does it mean?
15 ÷ 6 = 2.5, so (1, 2.5): 2.5 pages each minute.

4 Equations y = kx NC.7.RP.2

📖 Words to know
y = kx: the equation of a proportional relationship. k is the constant of proportionality.
📌 THE BIG IDEA: Find k (the unit rate, y ÷ x). Then write y = kx. Use it to find y for any x, or x for any y.

Gas costs $3.50 per gallon: y = ?x

A table has (4, 28) and (6, 42): k = ?, so y = ?

Example: a printer prints 84 pages in 12 minutes. Write the equation and find the pages in 15 minutes.

1k = 84 ÷ 12 = 7 pages per minute
2y = 7x (y = pages, x = minutes)
3x = 15: y = 7(15) = 105
4105 pages
⚠ Watch out: k is y ÷ x, not x ÷ y. Check: put a point in. 84 = 7(12) ✓
A machine fills 36 bottles in 3 minutes. In y = kx (y = bottles, x = minutes), what is k?
36 ÷ 3 = 12, so y = 12x.

5 Solving Proportional Problems NC.7.RP.2

📖 Words to know
Proportion: two equal ratios, like 3/4 = 9/12.
📌 THE BIG IDEA: Three ways to solve: 1 find the unit rate and multiply, 2 scale up a table or double number line, 3 write y = kx. Always check that your answer is reasonable.
3 cups of water for 2 cups of rice. For 8 cups of rice: k = 3/2, so (3/2) × 8 = 12 cups of water.

Example: 6 cans of juice cost $4.50. How much do 10 cans cost?

1Unit rate: 4.50 ÷ 6 = 0.75 dollars per can
2y = 0.75x
310 cans: 0.75 × 10 = 7.50
4$7.50. Reasonable: 10 cans cost more than 6 cans ✓
⚠ Watch out: Keep the same units on the top and bottom of both ratios.
A car uses 3 gallons of gas to go 45 miles. How many miles can it go on 8 gallons?
45 ÷ 3 = 15 miles per gallon. 15 × 8 = 120 miles.
A map shows 1/4 inch for every 1/6 mile. How many miles is 1 inch?
(1/6) ÷ (1/4) = (1/6) × 4 = 4/6 = 2/3 mile per inch.
Store A sells 6 lb of rice for $9. Store B sells 9 lb for $12.60. How many CENTS less per pound is the cheaper store?
A: 9 ÷ 6 = $1.50 per lb. B: 12.60 ÷ 9 = $1.40 per lb. B is $0.10 = 10 cents less per pound.
