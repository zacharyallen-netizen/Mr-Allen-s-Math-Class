Tips for a good fit

  • Follow the direction of the dots (up or down).
  • Put about the same number of dots above and below the line.
  • Ignore one odd dot. Fit the overall trend.

Closeness compares your line with the least-squares line Desmos finds (100 = just as good). Atomic masses are from the periodic table. The other data sets are made up for practice.
