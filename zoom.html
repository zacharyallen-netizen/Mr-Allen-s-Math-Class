Powers of Ten Zoom

Put objects in order from smallest to largest, from the nucleus of an atom to the Milky Way. Compare the exponents first.

NC.8.EE.3 · Science PS.8.1.2, ESS.8.1.1

How to compare

  • Bigger exponent → bigger number. 10⁵ is more than 10³.
  • Negative exponents are small. 10⁻¹⁰ is smaller than 10⁻⁷.
  • Same exponent? Compare the first numbers: 8 × 10⁻⁶ is more than 2 × 10⁻⁶.

Sizes are approximate (width, length or height in meters).
