Math Connections

Math in Financial Literacy

Use the math from class to make smart money choices: functions, slope, systems, exponents and scatter plots.

Each lesson is a short tutorial (about 6 minutes) and an assignment. When you finish, show Mr. Allen your completion screen. All money examples are made up for math practice, except the Lesson 7 data from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. This is not financial advice.
