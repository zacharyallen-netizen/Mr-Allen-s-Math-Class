=====================================================
  HOW TO PUT IT LIVE  (GitHub Desktop — the easy way)
=====================================================

This is the same method that worked before. It cannot flatten your
folders, which was the problem with drag-and-drop.

STEP 1.  Unzip this file on your computer.
         You'll get a folder called "website" containing:
           index.html, class-information.html, theme.css,
           HOW_TO_UPDATE.txt, and two folders: grade8 and math1

STEP 2.  Open GitHub Desktop. Make sure your repository
         "Mr-Allen-s-Math-Class" is the current one
         (top-left "Current repository").

STEP 3.  Click in GitHub Desktop:  Repository menu -> Show in Finder
         (Mac) or Show in Explorer (Windows).
         This opens your repo folder on your computer.

STEP 4.  In that repo folder, select everything EXCEPT the hidden
         ".git" folder, and delete it.
         (If you don't see a ".git" folder, that's fine — it's hidden.
          Just delete all the visible files and folders.)

STEP 5.  Open the "website" folder you unzipped in Step 1.
         Select EVERYTHING inside it (all files + the grade8 and
         math1 folders) and COPY it.

STEP 6.  Paste it all into your (now empty) repo folder.

STEP 7.  Go back to GitHub Desktop. It will show a long list of
         changes. In the "Summary" box at the bottom-left, type
         anything (like: update).
         Click "Commit to main", then click "Push origin" at the top.

STEP 8.  Wait about 2 minutes, then open your site to check:
         https://zacharyallen-netizen.github.io/Mr-Allen-s-Math-Class/

=====================================================
  YOUR "MEET MR. ALLEN" PAGE  (about.html)
=====================================================

The page works right now with what we already have: your welcome
letter, how you teach, what a class looks like, and your email.
You can add more about yourself any time. Nothing shows up until
you fill it in.

STEP 1.  Open the file  about.html  in any text editor.

STEP 2.  Scroll near the bottom and find the line:
             const BIO = {

STEP 3.  Type your words between the quote marks. For example:
             yearsTeaching: "8",
             interests: "Write your hobbies here.",

         The boxes you can fill in:
           photo ......... a picture, like "images/mr-allen.jpg"
                           (add the picture to your repo first;
                            square photos look best)
           background .... a few sentences about you
           education ..... your degrees or training
           yearsTeaching . a number like "8" (shows as "8 years")
           interests ..... what you like to do outside of school
           favoriteMath .. your favorite math idea, and why
           funFact ....... one fun fact about you

STEP 4.  Save, then re-deploy (same steps as above).

GOOD TO KNOW:
- Any box left as ""  stays hidden. No empty boxes, no "TBD".
- With no photo, the page shows a navy circle with "ZA".
- Keep the quote marks and the comma at the end of each line.
- Need a quote mark inside your text? Type  \"  instead.


============================================================
CHANGE THE LINKS IN THE TOP BAR (every page)
============================================================
The navy bar at the top of every page comes from ONE file:
    site-bar.js   (in the main folder, next to index.html)

STEP 1.  Open site-bar.js on GitHub and click the pencil.
STEP 2.  Near the top is a list called LINKS. Each line is one link:
           { label: "Arcade", href: "grade8/arcade.html" },
         label .. the words in the bar
         href ... the page, written from the main folder
STEP 3.  Add, remove or reorder lines. Keep the commas.
STEP 4.  Save. Every page on the site updates at once.

GOOD TO KNOW:
- The look of the hub pages (home, unit pages, class info) is in site.css.
- Notes, practice pages and games keep their own look.


============================================================
PRE-TEST AND PRACTICE SCORES ("Send my work")
============================================================
Every pre-test and unit practice page ends with a "Send my work to
Mr. Allen" box. It sends to the SAME Score Tracker as the Fluency Lab.

The address lives in two files — if you ever make a NEW deployment,
paste the new /exec address in both:
    tracker-config.js                                   (pre-tests, practice)
    grade8/Fluency-Lab-Package/fluency-lab/fluency-config.js  (Fluency Lab)

The Sheet's tabs (Standards, Statistics, Settings, Read Me) explain
themselves. Reports refresh every hour.


============================================================
BENCHMARK RESULTS (separate from the Score Tracker)
============================================================
The interactive benchmark reviews (8th Grade home → Benchmarks:
the Q1 Benchmark Review and the NC Check-In Benchmark Review)
send results to their OWN Google Sheet, "Benchmark Results —
Mr. Allen's Math Class". Nothing from the Fluency Lab, pre-tests or
practice goes there, and benchmarks never go to the Score Tracker.

SET IT UP ONCE (about five minutes)
STEP 1.  In Google Drive, make a new blank Google Sheet. Name it
         "Benchmark Results". Then click Extensions -> Apps Script.
         (If script.google.com -> New project gives an "internal
         error", this way avoids it.)
STEP 2.  Open _build/benchmark/BenchmarkTracker.gs on GitHub, copy all
         of it, and paste it over everything in the editor. Save
         (disk icon). Name the project "Benchmark Tracker".
STEP 3.  In the dropdown at the top choose  setup  and press  Run.
         Approve when Google asks. The new Sheet is in your Drive.
STEP 4.  Deploy -> New deployment -> gear icon -> Web app.
           Execute as: Me      Who has access: Anyone
         Click Deploy. Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec).
STEP 5.  Open benchmark-config.js (main folder) on GitHub, click the
         pencil, and paste the URL between the quote marks after
         ENDPOINT. Commit. (Or send the URL to Claude.)

Until Step 5 is done, students still get their score and feedback,
and the page tells them their results were not sent.

THE SHEET'S TABS
  Gradebook ........... best %, latest %, attempts, % on each standard
  Submissions ......... one row each time a student taps Submit
  Responses ........... every answer each student gave
  Question Analysis ... % right on each question (first attempts)
                        and the most common wrong answer
  Read Me ............. what each tab means
