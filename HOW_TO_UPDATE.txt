HOW CHANGES GO LIVE
============================================================
The website is the files in your GitHub repository. When a change is
saved there ("committed to main"), the site updates in about 2 minutes.

Three ways to make a change:
1. Ask Claude. Claude edits the files, checks them, and pushes them
   to main for you. This is the usual way.
2. Small word changes yourself: open the file on GitHub, click the
   pencil, change the words, then click "Commit changes".
3. Add a file (like a PDF): on GitHub open the folder (for example
   grade8), click "Add file" -> "Upload files", then commit.

You no longer need the zip file or the delete-and-paste steps.
After a change, wait 2 minutes and refresh the page
(Ctrl+Shift+R on a Chromebook clears the old copy). Claude adds a
version number to shared scripts when it changes them, so students
get the new version without clearing anything.

STEP-BY-STEP WORKSPACE (equations and angles)
============================================================
On Units 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 9 homework, pre-tests and practice, problems that
solve an equation have a "Work it out step by step" button. Students:
  1. Pick a move: Distribute, Combine like terms, or Add / Subtract /
     Multiply / Divide both sides (and type the amount).
  2. See the move on the equation: what cancels (crossed out), which
     terms are alike (same color), how a reciprocal makes 1.
  3. Write the new line themselves. Each line is checked right away,
     and the feedback names the mistake ("multiply every term inside",
     "flip the sign", "do the same to both sides"...).
  4. When x is alone, it checks the answer in the first line, and can
     put the answer in the answer box.
Unit 5 adds "Find the squares" and "Square root both sides": it explains
that √ undoes the square, that a length uses the positive root, and
accepts exact (√13) or rounded (≈ 3.6) answers.
Unit 2 (exponent rules) works the same way: students pick a rule
(multiply the numbers, add or subtract exponents, power of a power,
power of a product, zero or negative exponent, fix scientific
notation), see it on the expression, and write the next line. It
checks that the final answer uses positive exponents, each base once,
and a first number from 1 to 10 in scientific notation.
Unit 6 solves for y: 2x + y = 9 or y - 1 = 4(x - 2) becomes y = mx + b.
x-terms are green, and the feedback says when an x-term and a number
were combined, or which part (the x-term or the number) is wrong. For
point-slope problems, students write y - y1 = m(x - x1) first. "Find b"
problems start with an equation like 11 = 2(3) + b.
Word and angle problems start with "Write an equation" (any correct
equation is accepted; a wrong one gets a hint), then solve, then find
the angles. It never shows the answer. It is hidden when printing.
Made to feel calm (Nov 2026): move buttons in two columns on phones,
a small color key with only the colors on that line, and tap keys only
for symbols a Chromebook can't type (≤ ≥ ² √ π). Wrong lines start with
"Not quite." and say what to fix; after 3 tries it adds a short "take a
breath" note. Right lines get short praise.
Homework still sends to your Google Sheet exactly as before.

Files: grade8/step-work.js (the workspace) and grade8/homework-steps.js
(which homework problems get it). For a new homework, ask Claude to
"add the step-by-step workspace to Homework 5.2".

THE MATH 1 PASSWORD
============================================================
The password is not written anywhere on the site anymore (the files
are public on GitHub). The gate only keeps a scrambled "fingerprint"
of it. Ask Claude if you forget it.

- Students type it once. Then they can browse Math 1 until they
  close the browser. Capital letters don't matter.

  and silly messages for 12 seconds. It's a joke; nothing is sent.

TO CHANGE THE PASSWORD
STEP 1.  Open  .../Mr-Allen-s-Math-Class/math1/gate.html#new-password
STEP 2.  Type the new password and click "Make the code".
STEP 3.  Copy the long code. Open math1/gate.html on GitHub, click the
         pencil, and paste it between the quote marks after PW_HASH.
         Commit. (Or just send the code to Claude.)

IMPORTANT: this gate keeps honest students out. It is not real
security. Anything that must stay truly private belongs in Google

RESET ROOM (grade8/reset-room.html)  — link in the top bar
============================================================
A calm place for a short reset. The first activity is RIPPLES
(grade8/ripples.html): students tap the water to make soft notes that
ripple, repeat and fade. They pick 1, 2 or 3 minutes; a clock counts
down. A breathing circle (4 in, 4 out) can be turned on or off.
Before and after, they rate how they feel from 1 to 5 and get a short,
kind message. Check-in answers are never saved or sent.
"Change colors, sounds and speed" (closed at first) has: 6 color
themes, speed, 4 sounds, 3 moods, a background (heartbeat, soft beat,
rain, low hum), how many times a note repeats, and two volume sliders.
Settings are remembered on that Chromebook only.
DRIFT (grade8/drift.html) is calm focus music for work time: soft
chords, a slow steady pulse and quiet repeating notes, made fresh on
the Chromebook (no ads, no account, nothing sent). Students press
Play, then switch back to their work tab; the music keeps playing as
long as the Drift tab stays open. (A browser stops the sound when you
leave the page, so Drift always opens in its own tab, and while it
plays, links on the Drift page open in a new tab.)
Big Play/Pause and Mute buttons (the M key mutes too) and a volume
slider. "Change the music" has: mood (Bright, Deep, Dreamy), pace
(Slow, Medium, Steady), pulse (Off, Soft, Steady), melody on/off, an
optional 10/20/30-minute focus timer that fades out, and glow colors.
The Reset Room page says: ask Mr. Allen first, use headphones, check in,
then go back to work. To change those words or add a new activity,
ask Claude.
