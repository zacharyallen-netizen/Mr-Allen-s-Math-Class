LASER VAULT

Break into the vault. Every door is locked by a laser. Find the right angle, aim the turret, and hit the crystal to open the door.

1 · Pick a wing

How to play. Move through the maze with the arrow keys, or tap a room next to you. A glowing bar is a locked door. Walk into it to try the lock. Each lock shows a figure drawn to scale. Find the ? angle (or x), type it, and press FIRE. The turret turns by your angle. If you are right, the beam hits the crystal and the door opens.
Gold doors are harder shortcuts. Pink doors hide bonus crystals. The Core door has two locks. Stuck? Use Hint. After two misses you can work it out step by step.
