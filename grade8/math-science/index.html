How it works

Each lesson has a 5–8 minute tutorial with "Try it" checks, then 8 assignment questions like the NC Check-Ins. You get two tries on each question. When you finish, type your name to see your score and a verification code. Take a screenshot or print your results for Mr. Allen.

Science standards: 2023 NC Standard Course of Study, grade 8. Math standards: NC.8.
