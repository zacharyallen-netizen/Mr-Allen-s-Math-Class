Name: Date: Period:
SCORE3.0Basic Understanding

NC.8.G.5 Understand angle relationships at a point and on a line.

• I can find missing angles using supplementary, complementary, vertical, and linear-pair relationships.

Match each vocabulary term to its definition, then find each missing angle.

supplementary angles A. Two angles whose measures add to 90°.
complementary angles B. Opposite angles formed by two intersecting lines; they are equal.
vertical angles C. A line that crosses two or more other lines.
transversal D. Two angles whose measures add to 180°.
corresponding angles E. Matching-position angles at two intersections; equal when the lines are parallel.
exterior angle F. The angle formed outside a triangle when one side is extended.

Angle facts — reference

Straight line = 180° · Triangle angles sum to 180° · Right angle = 90°

Exterior angle of a triangle = sum of the two remote interior angles.
