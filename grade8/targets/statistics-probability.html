A coach tracked push-ups in one minute after each week of training. A line of best fit goes through (1, 22) and (6, 42). What is the equation of the line?
Hint: Find the slope first, then put one point into y = mx + b to find b.
How to solve it
  1. Slope: m = (42 − 22) ÷ (6 − 1) = 20 ÷ 5 = 4.
  2. Use (1, 22): 22 = 4(1) + b.
  3. b = 22 − 4 = 18, so y = 4x + 18.

  1. y-intercept = 24: the candle is 24 cm tall before it is lit.

Students counted cricket chirps per minute at different temperatures. A line of best fit passes through (60, 80) and (80, 160). Which equation and prediction for 72°F are correct?
Hint: Find the slope, then use one point to find b. Then put in x = 72.
How to solve it
  1. Slope: (160 − 80) ÷ (80 − 60) = 80 ÷ 20 = 4, so about 4 more chirps per minute for each 1°F.

  4. At 72°F: 4(72) − 160 = 288 − 160 = 128 chirps per minute.

Two-way tables

Priority · Unit 7 · 0 / 8 solved

Explore it: Build a two-way table — Change the counts and watch the totals and relative frequencies update.

Approaching · score 3

SP.4-1Approaching (3)✓ Solved

I can construct a two-way table with row totals, column totals, and a grand total from a description.

A librarian asked 90 students if they prefer books on paper or on a screen. 50 were 7th graders and the rest were 8th graders. 30 of the 7th graders prefer paper. 18 of the 8th graders prefer a screen. Which two-way table is correct?
Hint: Start with the grand total of 90, then find the 8th grade total.
How to solve it
  1. 8th grade total = 90 − 50 = 40.
  2. 7th grade screen = 50 − 30 = 20. 8th grade paper = 40 − 18 = 22.
  3. Column totals: paper = 30 + 22 = 52; screen = 20 + 18 = 38.
  4. Check: 52 + 38 = 90.

A school surveyed students about eating breakfast. How many 7th graders skip breakfast, and what kind of frequency is it?
Hint: Find where the 7th grade row and the Skips breakfast column cross.
How to solve it
  1. Go across the 7th grade row to the Skips breakfast column: 25.
  2. This count is inside the table, for one row and one column, so it is a joint frequency.
  3. Totals on the edges (like 75 or 60) are marginal frequencies.
