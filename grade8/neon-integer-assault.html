Neon Integer Assault

Invaders march down. A number centipede winds through the mushroom field. Only some numbers answer your equations.

How to play
Equations appear at the top. Each invader and centipede segment carries a number. Shoot only the numbers that answer an equation. A right hit destroys it and solves the equation. A wrong hit does not hurt it, and it fires back.
Move: ← → or A D (or drag). Fire: Space (or tap). Pause: P or Esc. Dodge the bombs and do not let the centipede reach your ship.
InvadersThey march side to side and step down. They drop bombs.
CentipedeHit a right segment and it turns into a mushroom. Mushrooms block shots.
Streaks5 right in a row = +1 armor. 6 in a row = OVERDRIVE (fast fire).
