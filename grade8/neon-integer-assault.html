📋 How to Play
An equation appears at the top of the screen. Tap (or click) the number that makes the equation true before it reaches you. Correct hits raise your score and accuracy; wrong ones cost armor. Press P or Esc to pause.

Blast the targets that solve the equations. Clean streaks charge bonus armor, bad hits crack your shield, and each wave adds more pressure.

TargetOnly shoot numbers that match an unsolved equation.
ComboConsecutive correct hits multiply your score.
