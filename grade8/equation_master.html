🎮 How It Works

  • You'll see an equation and choose the correct next step to solve it.
  • When you choose correctly, the equation updates to show exactly what changed — terms cancelling, combining, distributing.
  • Keep picking the right moves until you reach the solution!
  • A wrong move gets crossed out and the beast strikes. Read why, then pick again. You only move on with the right move.
  • Take a moment: the buttons unlock after you read the equation. Points only count for moves you get right on the first try.
  • Get 70% of the moves right on the first try to unlock the next level.
