1 Scientific Notation

📖 Words to know
Standard form: a number written out with all its digits, like 45,000,000.
Scientific notation: a number written as (a number from 1 up to 10) × 10 to a power, like 4.5 × 10⁷.
Coefficient: the first number in scientific notation. In 4.5 × 10⁷, the coefficient is 4.5.
📌 THE FORMAT: A number in scientific notation looks like 4.5 × 10⁷. The first number must be at least 1 and less than 10. The base is always 10.

Move the Decimal

The gold dot is where the decimal point lands: right after the first digit that is not 0. Each hop is one power of 10.

Divide coefficients: click to reveal 8 ÷ 4 = 2

⚠️ ADJUSTMENT RULES: If coefficient ≥ 10: move decimal LEFT, ADD 1 to exponent. If coefficient < 1: move decimal RIGHT, SUBTRACT 1 from exponent.

Only coefficients in the gold band (1 up to, not including, 10) are finished. 20 and 0.3 still need one more hop.

Example needing adjustment: (5 × 10³) × (4 × 10²)

Multiply: click to reveal 5 × 4 = 20
Add exponents: click to reveal 3 + 2 = 5 → result: 20 × 10⁵
Adjust (20 ≥ 10): click to reveal 20 = 2.0 × 10¹, so ADD 1 to the exponent
✓ Final answer: click to reveal 2.0 × 10⁶
ERROR ANALYSIS Convert 0.0045 to scientific notation. A student wrote 4.5 × 10³. What's the mistake?

0.0045 is less than 1, and the point hops to the RIGHT. Which sign must the exponent have?

Small number → NEGATIVE exponent. Correct: 4.5 × 10⁻³. Numbers less than 1 always have negative exponents in scientific notation.

C. Word Problems

1. The Sun is about 9.3 × 10⁷ miles from Earth. Light travels 1.86 × 10⁵ miles per second. How many seconds does it take light to reach Earth?

Each tick is 10 times the one before. How many 1-second trips fit in the whole distance?

(9.3 × 10⁷) ÷ (1.86 × 10⁵) = 5 × 10² seconds (500 seconds).
2. A bacterium is about 2 × 10⁻⁶ m long. A virus is about 5 × 10⁻⁸ m long. How many times longer is the bacterium?

The bacterium is further right, so it is longer. Divide to find how many times.

(2 × 10⁻⁶) ÷ (5 × 10⁻⁸) = 0.4 × 10² = 40 times longer.
3. The US national debt is about 3.4 × 10¹³ dollars. The US population is about 3.4 × 10⁸ people. What is the debt per person?

Split the whole debt into one equal share per person.

(3.4 × 10¹³) ÷ (3.4 × 10⁸) = 1 × 10⁵ = $100,000 per person.
4. A grain of sand has mass 6.5 × 10⁻⁴ grams. A beach has about 8 × 10¹⁸ grains of sand. What is the total mass in grams?

Same mass, many times: multiply.

(6.5 × 10⁻⁴)(8 × 10¹⁸) = 52 × 10¹⁴ = 5.2 × 10¹⁵ grams.
5. There are about 3.7 × 10¹³ cells in a human body. Each cell is about 1 × 10⁻⁵ m wide. If all cells were lined up, how long in meters?

Same width, many times: multiply.

(3.7 × 10¹³)(1 × 10⁻⁵) = 3.7 × 10⁸ meters.
6. A computer does 4.8 × 10⁹ calculations per second. A task needs 1.44 × 10¹² calculations. How many seconds to finish?

Cut the whole task into 1-second pieces. How many pieces?

(1.44 × 10¹²) ÷ (4.8 × 10⁹) = 0.3 × 10³ = 3 × 10² seconds (300 seconds).
