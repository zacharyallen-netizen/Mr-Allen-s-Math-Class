Unit 5 — Pythagorean Theorem

Jan 5 – Jan 21

Standards: NC.8.G.6–8

U1 U2 U3 U4 U5 U6 U7 U8 U9

The Pythagorean Theorem and its converse, missing sides, word problems, and distance on the coordinate plane.

What you will learn in Unit 5

Learning targets

  • I can use the Pythagorean Theorem to find a missing side of a right triangle
  • I can apply its converse to test whether a triangle is right
  • I can solve real-world and coordinate-distance problems

Standards

  • 8.G.6Explain the Pythagorean Theorem and its converse.
  • 8.G.7Use the Pythagorean Theorem to find missing sides in 2-D and 3-D.
  • 8.G.8Use the Pythagorean Theorem to find the distance between two points.
