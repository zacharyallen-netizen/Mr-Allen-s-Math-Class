Unit 7 — Data Analysis & Statistics

Mar 3 – Mar 19

Standards: NC.8.SP.1–4

U1 U2 U3 U4 U5 U6 U7 U8 U9

Scatter plots, association, lines of best fit, and two-way tables.

What you will learn in Unit 7

Learning targets

  • I can describe association in a scatter plot
  • I can draw and use a line of best fit to make predictions
  • I can read and build two-way tables and relative frequencies

Standards

  • 8.SP.1Make and read scatter plots and describe patterns.
  • 8.SP.2Draw a line of best fit and judge how well it fits.
  • 8.SP.3Use a line of best fit to make predictions and explain slope.
  • 8.SP.4Make and read two-way tables.
