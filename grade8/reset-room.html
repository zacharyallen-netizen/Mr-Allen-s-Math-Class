Reset Room

Feeling stressed, stuck or frustrated? Take a short break to reset, then come back ready to learn.

How to use the Reset Room

  1. Ask Mr. Allen first, and put on headphones.
  2. Pick an activity. Each one takes 3 minutes or less.
  3. Check in before and after: how do you feel from 1 to 5?
  4. When the time is up, go back to your work. If you still feel stuck, tell Mr. Allen.

Activities

Ripples

Tap the water to make soft notes that ripple, repeat and fade. Breathe along with the glowing circle. Change the colors, sounds and speed, or add a calm background beat.

Why a Reset Room?

Knowing when you need a reset, and using a strategy to get back on track, is part of Self-Regulation, one of our school's durable skills. See the Self-Regulation scale.
