Translations · Reflections · Rotations · Dilations · Congruence & Similarity · NC.8.G.2–4

Reflection: x-axis → (x, −y) · y-axis → (−x, y) · y=x → (y, x)

90° CW: (x,y)→(y,−x) · 90° CCW: (x,y)→(−y,x) · 180°: (x,y)→(−x,−y) · Dilation: (kx, ky)

💡 Tip: Translations, reflections & rotations are rigid motions (preserve size → congruent). Dilations change size (→ similar).
➡️ Translations (NC.8.G.3)
Problem 8A
blue = pre-image · gold dashed = image
Triangle ABC has vertices A(1, 1), B(4, 1), C(1, 4). It is translated by the rule (x + 3, y − 2). What are the coordinates of A′?
Add 3 to x, subtract 2 from y: A(1,1) → A′(1+3, 1−2) = (4, −1). Do the SAME rule to B and C to find the whole image triangle.
🪞 Reflections (NC.8.G.3)
Problem 8B
blue = pre-image · gold dashed = image
Triangle DEF has vertices D(2, 2), E(5, 2), F(2, 5). It is reflected over the x-axis. What are the coordinates of E′?
Reflecting over the x-axis keeps x the same and negates y: (x,y) → (x,−y). So E(5,2) → E′(5,−2).
Problem 8C
blue = pre-image · gold dashed = image
Quadrilateral GHIJ has vertices G(−5, 1), H(−2, 1), I(−2, 4), J(−5, 4). It's reflected over the y-axis. What is the image of H?
Reflecting over the y-axis keeps y the same and negates x: (x,y) → (−x,y). So H(−2,1) → H′(2,1).
Problem 8D
blue = pre-image · gold dashed = image
Triangle KLM has vertices K(1, 4), L(4, 4), M(1, 1). It is reflected over the line y = x. What is the image of K?
Reflecting over y = x SWAPS the coordinates: (x,y) → (y,x). So K(1,4) → K′(4,1).
🔄 Rotations (NC.8.G.3)
Problem 8E
blue = pre-image · gold dashed = image
Triangle NOP has vertices N(2, 2), O(5, 2), P(2, 5). It is rotated 90° clockwise about the origin. What is the image of O?
90° clockwise: (x,y) → (y,−x). So O(5,2) → O′(2,−5).
Problem 8F
blue = pre-image · gold dashed = image
Triangle QRS has vertices Q(−5, 2), R(−2, 2), S(−5, 5). It is rotated 180° about the origin. What is the image of R?
180° rotation: (x,y) → (−x,−y). So R(−2,2) → R′(2,−2).
Problem 8G
blue = pre-image · gold dashed = image
Triangle TUV has vertices T(1, −2), U(4, −2), V(1, −5). It is rotated 90° counter-clockwise about the origin. What is the image of U?
90° CCW: (x,y) → (−y,x). So U(4,−2) → U′(2,4).
🔍 Dilations (NC.8.G.3, NC.8.G.4)
Problem 8H
blue = pre-image · gold dashed = image
Triangle ABC has vertices A(1, 1), B(3, 1), C(1, 2). It is dilated by scale factor k = 2, centered at the origin (dashed gold triangle shown is the image). What is the image of B?
Dilation from the origin: multiply EACH coordinate by k. B(3,1) → B′(3×2, 1×2) = (6, 2).
Problem 8I
A triangle with 10 cm sides is dilated by scale factor k = ½. What is the new side length?
Multiply by the scale factor: 10 × ½ = 5 cm. A scale factor less than 1 makes the figure smaller (a reduction).
🧩 Congruence & Similarity (NC.8.G.2, NC.8.G.4)
Problem 8J
Which single transformation changes the SIZE of a figure (like the triangle shown growing larger)?
Only dilations change size! Translations, reflections, and rotations are all rigid motions that preserve size — they only move the figure, never resize it.
Problem 8K
original → moved → flipped
A figure is translated, then reflected (shown above — same triangle, just moved and flipped). The final figure is:
Translations and reflections are both rigid motions — they preserve size AND shape. A sequence of rigid motions always produces a figure congruent to the original.
Problem 8L
Triangle A has sides 3, 4, 5. Triangle B has sides 6, 8, 10 (shown above, scaled up). What single transformation maps A to B?
Every side doubled: 3→6, 4→8, 5→10 — that's a dilation with scale factor k = 2. The triangles are similar (same shape, different size), not congruent.

📝 Worked Examples

Study these step-by-step solutions before attempting the problems above.

Composite: translate △ABC — A(2,5), B(5,4), C(3,1) — by (x−3, y+1), then reflect over the x-axis
Step 1: Translate every vertex of △ABC by (x−3, y+1)
A(2,5)→A′(−1,6)   B(5,4)→B′(2,5)   C(3,1)→C′(0,2)
Step 2: Reflect every vertex over the x-axis: (x, −y)
A′(−1,6)→A″(−1,−6)   B′(2,5)→B″(2,−5)   C′(0,2)→C″(0,−2)
Step 3: Final image
△A″B″C″: A″(−1,−6)   B″(2,−5)   C″(0,−2) ✓
△ABC (solid blue) → translate → △A′B′C′ (dashed gold) → reflect over x-axis → △A″B″C″ (dashed green)

🔍 Common Mistakes

Can you spot what went wrong?

Rotate △JKL 90° CCW — vertex J is at (3,−2)
✗ Student: Student writes: (−2, 3)
✓ Fix: WRONG! Rule is (−y, x). y=−2, so −y = 2. Correct: (2, 3)
💡 Be careful with negatives in rotation rules. −(−2) = +2.
Dilate △FGH by k=½ — vertex F is at (4,6)
✗ Student: Student writes: (2, 8)
✓ Fix: WRONG! ½ × 4 = 2 ✓ but ½ × 6 = 3, not 8. Correct: (2, 3)
💡 Multiply BOTH coordinates by the scale factor.

⚡ Key Tips for This Unit

✅ Translation: (x+a, y+b) — slide. CONGRUENT.
✅ Reflection: x-axis → negate y. y-axis → negate x. CONGRUENT.
✅ Rotation 90° CCW: (−y, x). 180°: (−x,−y). 270° CCW: (y,−x). CONGRUENT.
✅ Dilation: (kx, ky). SIMILAR (not congruent unless k=1).
