Unit 2 — Exponents & Scientific Notation

Aug 27 – Sep 25

Standards: NC.8.EE.1 · NC.8.EE.3–4

U1 U2 U3 U4 U5 U6 U7 U8 U9

Exponent properties, negative & zero exponents, and operations with scientific notation.

What you will learn in Unit 2

Learning targets

  • I can apply the product, quotient, and power rules of exponents
  • I can simplify expressions with negative and zero exponents
  • I can write and compute with numbers in scientific notation

Standards

  • 8.EE.1Use the rules of exponents, including zero and negative exponents.
  • 8.EE.3Write very large and very small numbers in scientific notation.
  • 8.EE.4Add, subtract, multiply and divide in scientific notation.
