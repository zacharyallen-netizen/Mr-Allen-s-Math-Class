hypotenuse A. The two sides that form the right angle.
legs B. a² + b² = c², relating the sides of a right triangle.
Pythagorean theorem C. The side opposite the right angle; the longest side.
right triangle D. A triangle that contains one 90° angle.
square root E. A number that, multiplied by itself, gives a given value.
converse F. If a² + b² = c², then the triangle is a right triangle.

Pythagorean theorem — reference

a² + b² = c²

c is the hypotenuse (opposite the right angle); a and b are the legs.
