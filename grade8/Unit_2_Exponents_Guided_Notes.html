⚠️ Parentheses come first! A power attaches only to what it touches. −3² = −(3·3) = −9, but (−3)² = (−3)(−3) = 9. And a power written outside parentheses hits everything inside: (3x²)³ = 27x⁶ — the 3 is cubed too. Keep an eye on parentheses all unit long.

2 Combining Like Terms

📌 KEY IDEA: Like terms have the SAME variables with the SAME exponents. To combine, add or subtract the coefficients and keep the variable part the same.

See it in Action

Click each model to step through the reasoning.

Combine · 3x² + 5x² = 8x²
x²x²x²+x²x²x²x²x²→x²x²x²x²x²x²x²x²8x²
Unlike · 4x² + 3x cannot combine
x²x²x²x²+xxx→4x² + 3x

Watch Out

Different variables or exponents = NOT like terms:   5x + 3y stays 5x + 3y  ·  2x² + 4x stays 2x² + 4x
Longer expressions — group first:   4x² + 2x + 3x² − x = (4x² + 3x²) + (2x − x) = 7x² + x

Worked Example — Simplify 5a² + 3a − 2a² + 7a

Step 2 — Combine a²: click to reveal 5a² − 2a² = 3a²
Step 3 — Combine a: click to reveal 3a + 7a = 10a
✓ Combine: click to reveal 3a² + 10a

Negative Exponents Across the Fraction Bar

A negative exponent moves its factor to the OTHER side of the fraction bar and turns positive.

Move across the bar:   x⁻³ = 1x³  ·  1x⁻³ = x³  ·  x⁻²y⁻⁴ = y⁴x²
In parentheses — flip to the reciprocal:   (ab)⁻ⁿ = (ba)ⁿ.   Example:   (23)⁻² = (32)² = 94  ·  (x²y)⁻³ = (yx²)³ = y³x⁶

Multiplying — 3-Step Process

  1. ? the coefficients (the a-values)
  2. ? the exponents
  3. Adjust if coefficient is NOT between ? and ?

Example (no adjustment): (3 × 10⁴) × (2 × 10⁵)

Multiply coefficients: click to reveal 3 × 2 = 6
ADD exponents: click to reveal 4 + 5 = 9
✓ Answer: click to reveal 6 × 10⁹

Dividing — 3-Step Process

  1. ? the coefficients
  2. ? the exponents
  3. Adjust if needed

Example (no adjustment): (8 × 10⁶) ÷ (4 × 10²)

Divide coefficients: click to reveal 8 ÷ 4 = 2
SUBTRACT exponents: click to reveal 6 − 2 = 4
✓ Answer: click to reveal 2 × 10⁴
⚠️ ADJUSTMENT RULES: If coefficient ≥ 10: move decimal LEFT, ADD 1 to exponent. If coefficient < 1: move decimal RIGHT, SUBTRACT 1 from exponent.

Example needing adjustment: (5 × 10³) × (4 × 10²)

Multiply: click to reveal 5 × 4 = 20
Add exponents: click to reveal 3 + 2 = 5 → result: 20 × 10⁵
Adjust (20 ≥ 10): click to reveal 20 = 2.0 × 10¹, so ADD 1 to the exponent
✓ Final answer: click to reveal 2.0 × 10⁶

Small number → NEGATIVE exponent. Correct: 4.5 × 10⁻³. Numbers less than 1 always have negative exponents in scientific notation.

✏ Practice D — Word Problems

Identify the operation, write your setup, then adjust so 1 ≤ a < 10.

1. The Sun is about 9.3 × 10⁷ miles from Earth. Light travels 1.86 × 10⁵ miles per second. How many seconds does it take light to reach Earth?
(9.3 × 10⁷) ÷ (1.86 × 10⁵) = 5 × 10² seconds (500 seconds).
2. A bacteria cell is about 2 × 10⁻⁶ m long. A virus is about 5 × 10⁻⁸ m long. How many times longer is the bacteria?
(2 × 10⁻⁶) ÷ (5 × 10⁻⁸) = 40 times longer.
3. The US national debt is about 3.4 × 10¹³ dollars. The population is about 3.4 × 10⁸ people. What is the debt per person?
(3.4 × 10¹³) ÷ (3.4 × 10⁸) = 1 × 10⁵ = $100,000 per person.
4. A grain of sand has mass 6.5 × 10⁻⁴ grams. A beach has about 8 × 10¹⁸ grains. What is the total mass in grams?
(6.5 × 10⁻⁴)(8 × 10¹⁸) = 5.2 × 10¹⁵ grams.
5. There are about 3.7 × 10¹³ cells in a human body. Each cell is about 1 × 10⁻⁵ m wide. Lined up, how long in meters?
(3.7 × 10¹³)(1 × 10⁻⁵) = 3.7 × 10⁸ meters.
6. A computer does 4.8 × 10⁹ calculations per second. A task needs 1.44 × 10¹² calculations. How many seconds to finish?
(1.44 × 10¹²) ÷ (4.8 × 10⁹) = 3 × 10² seconds (300 seconds).
7. A hard drive holds 2 × 10¹² bytes. Each photo is 4 × 10⁶ bytes. How many photos fit?
(2 × 10¹²) ÷ (4 × 10⁶) = 5 × 10⁵ photos (500,000).
8. Light travels 3 × 10⁸ meters per second. How far does it travel in 6 × 10³ seconds?
(3 × 10⁸)(6 × 10³) = 1.8 × 10¹² meters.
