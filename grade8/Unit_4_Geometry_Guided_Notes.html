2 Parallel Lines & Transversals

📌 REMEMBER: Corresponding, Alternate Interior, Alternate Exterior, and Vertical angles are CONGRUENT (equal). Same-Side Interior and Same-Side Exterior angles are SUPPLEMENTARY (add to 180°).

Two parallel lines (m ∥ n) cut by transversal t form 8 angles. Corresponding: (1,5)(2,6)(3,7)(4,8) · Alt. Interior: (3,6)(4,5) · Alt. Exterior: (1,8)(2,7) · Same-Side Interior: (3,5)(4,6)

3 Triangle Theorems

🔑 TRIANGLE ANGLE SUM: The three interior angles of a triangle always add up to 180°.
🔑 EXTERIOR ANGLE THEOREM: An exterior angle equals the SUM of the two non-adjacent (remote) interior angles.

Problem 1 — Triangle Angle Sum: x + 72 + 55 = 180

Solve: click to reveal x + 127 = 180 → x = 53°

Problem 2 — Exterior Angle Theorem: x + 65 = 140

Solve: click to reveal x + 65 = 140 → x = 75°
ERROR ANALYSIS Jordan's Exterior Angle Mistake — A triangle has interior angles 35° and 70°. Exterior angle = x. Jordan wrote x = 70 − 35 = 35°.
Jordan subtracted instead of adding. The exterior angle = SUM of the remote interior angles: x = 35 + 70 = 105°.

4 Angle-Angle (AA) Similarity

🔑 SIMILAR TRIANGLES: Two triangles are similar (written △ABC ~ △DEF) when they have the same shape but not necessarily the same size. Corresponding angles are equal and corresponding sides are in proportion.
🔑 ANGLE-ANGLE (AA) CRITERION: If two angles of one triangle are equal to two angles of another triangle, then the triangles are similar. You only ever need to check TWO pairs of angles.
🔑 WHY TWO ANGLES ARE ENOUGH: Every triangle's angles add to 180°. Once two pairs of angles match, the third pair has no choice but to match as well — so all three pairs are equal.
📌 WATCH OUT: AA needs two pairs of angles that match each other — not simply two angles given in each triangle.

Problem 1 — Are they similar? ∠A = 50°, ∠B = 60° and ∠D = 50°, ∠E = 60°

Decide: click to reveal Two pairs of angles are equal (50° = 50° and 60° = 60°), so by AA the triangles are similar: △ABC ~ △DEF.

Problem 2 — △ABC ~ △DEF with ∠A = 35° and ∠B = 85°. Find ∠F.

Solve: click to reveal ∠C = 180 − 35 − 85 = 60°. ∠F corresponds to ∠C, so ∠F = 60°.

Problem 3 — Similar triangles: sides 6 and 8 match sides 9 and x. Find x.

Solve: click to reveal Scale factor = 9 ÷ 6 = 1.5, so x = 8 × 1.5 = 12.
ERROR ANALYSIS Riley's AA Mistake — Triangle 1 has angles 40° and 75°. Triangle 2 has angles 40° and 70°. Riley wrote “Each triangle shows two angles, so they are similar by AA.”
AA needs two pairs of angles that match each other. Only the 40° angles match here. Triangle 1's angles are 40°, 75°, 65°; Triangle 2's are 40°, 70°, 70°. The two sets are different, so the triangles are not similar.
