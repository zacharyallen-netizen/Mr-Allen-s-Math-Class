NC.8.EE.7 · NC.8.G.5 Solve one- and two-step equations and inequalities. Find angles with one step.

• I can solve simple equations and inequalities, and use angle pairs and the triangle sum to find a missing angle.

Match each vocabulary term to its definition. Then answer each question.

transversal A. Angles between the parallel lines, on the same side of the transversal. They add to 180°.
corresponding angles B. An equation that is never true, for any value of x.
alternate interior angles C. A line that crosses two or more other lines.
same-side interior angles D. Angles in the same position at each crossing. They are equal when the lines are parallel.
exterior angle E. An angle outside a triangle, made by extending one side.
no solution F. Angles between the parallel lines, on opposite sides of the transversal. They are equal.

Reference

Equal: corresponding, alternate interior, alternate exterior · Add to 180°: same-side interior, linear pair, the 3 angles of a triangle

Exterior angle = sum of the two remote interior angles. Multiply or divide by a negative → flip the inequality sign.
