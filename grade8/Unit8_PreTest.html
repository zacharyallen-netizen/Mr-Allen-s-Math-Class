SCORE3.0Basic Understanding

NC.8.G.1 Identify transformations and translate figures.

• I can name translations, reflections, and rotations and translate a point.

Match each vocabulary term to its definition, then find each image point.

translation A. A flip of a figure over a line.
reflection B. A slide; every point moves the same distance and direction.
rotation C. A turn of a figure about a fixed point.
dilation D. An enlargement or reduction by a scale factor.
congruent E. Same size and same shape.
image F. The resulting figure after a transformation.

Coordinate rules — reference

Reflect over x-axis: (x, y) → (x, −y) · Reflect over y-axis: (x, y) → (−x, y)
Rotate 180°: (x, y) → (−x, −y) · Rotate 90° CCW: (x, y) → (−y, x) · Rotate 90° CW: (x, y) → (y, −x)
Dilation (center origin), scale k: (x, y) → (kx, ky)
