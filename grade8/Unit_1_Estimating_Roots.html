2 The 4-step algorithm

Same idea, but it gives you a decimal to the nearest tenth. It works the same for cube roots — just use cubes.

🔎 Check by squaring: the algorithm gets you close. To be sure of the tenth, square (or cube) your answer — it should land just under your number. Example: 6.7² = 44.89 and 6.8² = 46.24, so √45 rounds to 6.7.

📌 Mixed number → decimal: whole + (numerator ÷ denominator). Example: 3⅖ = 3 + (2÷5) = 3.4.   Line up decimals to the same place before you compare — 2.25 vs 2.3 is easy to misread quickly.
