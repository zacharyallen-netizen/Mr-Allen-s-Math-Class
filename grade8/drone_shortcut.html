Drone Shortcut

You fly a delivery drone over a city grid. Streets go only across and up, but the drone flies straight — find the shortcut.

a2 + b2 = c2a and b are the legs. c is the hypotenuse, the straight flight.
How to play
  1. Pick a delivery. Three drop-offs wait on the map. Estimate which flight is shortest. A good guess earns a bonus.
  2. Find the flight. Find the legs. Square them and add. Then take the square root to get c.
  3. Save battery. Each flight uses battery equal to its length. Short flights let you finish all five deliveries.
Levels

Standards: NC.8.G.6 (explain the theorem and its converse), NC.8.G.7 (find unknown sides in 2D and 3D), NC.8.G.8 (distance between two points).
