Part 1 · NC.8.G.5 · Unit 4

Angle Relationships

Big idea

When angles share a side or two lines cross, the angles are tied together. If you know one, you can find the others.

Words to know

Adjacent anglesTwo angles that share a vertex and a side. They sit side by side.
Vertical anglesAngles across from each other where two lines cross. They are equal.
Linear pairTwo adjacent angles that make a straight line. They add to 180°.
ComplementaryTwo angles that add to 90°. Together they make a right angle.
SupplementaryTwo angles that add to 180°. Together they make a straight line.
Right angleAn angle of exactly 90°. A small square marks it.

Look at how the angles touch. That tells you the rule.

RelationshipWhat it looks likeRule
Vertical anglesAcross from each other, like an Xequal
Linear pairSide by side on a straight lineadd to 180°
SupplementaryTwo angles that make a straight angleadd to 180°
ComplementaryTwo angles that fill a right angleadd to 90°
Memory tip: Complementary comes before Supplementary in the alphabet, and 90 comes before 180.
Example 1 · Two lines cross

Two lines cross. ∠1 = 58°. Find ∠2, ∠3 and ∠4.

  • ∠3 is across from ∠1. Vertical angles are equal, so ∠3 = 58°.
  • ∠1 and ∠2 make a straight line. 180 − 58 = 122, so ∠2 = 122°.
  • ∠4 is across from ∠2, so ∠4 = 122°.

Check: 58 + 122 + 58 + 122 = 360. All the way around is 360°.

Example 2 · Solve for x

The two angles fill a right angle. Find x and each angle.

  • They fill a right angle, so they are complementary. Write: (x + 20) + (2x + 10) = 90
  • Combine like terms: 3x + 30 = 90
  • Subtract 30: 3x = 60. Divide by 3: x = 20
  • Plug in: x + 20 = 40° and 2x + 10 = 50°. Check: 40 + 50 = 90 ✓
Try it · Turn a line

Move the slider to turn one line. Watch all four angles. Which ones always match? Which ones always add to 180°?

Skills you need
  • Name a pair as vertical, linear pair, complementary or supplementary.
  • Use the rule to find a missing angle.
  • Write and solve an equation to find x.
  • Plug x back in to find each angle.
Common mistakes
  • Mistake: Setting vertical angles to add to 180°.Fix: Vertical angles are across the X. They are equal.
  • Mistake: Using 180° for complementary angles.Fix: Complementary = 90° (a right angle). Supplementary = 180°.
  • Mistake: Stopping at x.Fix: If the question asks for the angle, plug x back in.
