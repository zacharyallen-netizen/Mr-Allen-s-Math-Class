Multi-Step Equations

Solve in this order. 1. Distribute. 2. Combine like terms on each side. 3. Move the x-terms to one side. 4. Undo + and −. 5. Undo × and ÷. 6. Check your answer in the original.

Stuck? Every problem has a Solve it step by step button. Pick the best next step and watch the math happen.

How to read the colored steps
− 9 done to BOTH sides+ 9 − 9 cancels (makes 0)3x x-terms (like terms)12 numbers (like terms)3 number being distributed> sign flipped
Example: 4(x − 1) + 2x = 3x + 11
1Solve. 2x + 9 = 25
Solution
  1. Start
    2x + 9 = 25
  2. 1Subtract 9 from both sides.
    2x + 9 − 9 = 25 − 9
    2x = 16
    + 9 and − 9 cancel (they make 0). 25 − 9 = 16.
  3. 2Divide both sides by 2.
    2x2 = 162
    x = 8
    2 ÷ 2 = 1, so only x is left. 16 ÷ 2 = 8.
Check: 2(8) + 9 = 16 + 9 = 25 ✓
2Solve. 3(x + 4) = 27
Solution
  1. Start
    3(x + 4) = 27
  2. 1Distribute: multiply the 3 by EACH term inside.
    3 · x + 3 · 4 = 27
    3x + 12 = 27
    3 · x = 3x and 3 · 4 = 12.
  3. 2Subtract 12 from both sides.
    3x + 12 − 12 = 27 − 12
    3x = 15
    + 12 and − 12 cancel (they make 0). 27 − 12 = 15.
  4. 3Divide both sides by 3.
    3x3 = 153
    x = 5
    3 ÷ 3 = 1, so only x is left. 15 ÷ 3 = 5.
Check: 3(5 + 4) = 3(9) = 27 ✓
3Solve. 7x − 6 = 4x + 9
Solution
  1. Start
    7x − 6 = 4x + 9
  2. 1Subtract 4x from both sides.
    7x − 4x − 6 = 4x − 4x + 9
    3x − 6 = 9
    4x − 4x cancel on the right. On the left, 7x − 4x = 3x.
  3. 2Add 6 to both sides.
    3x − 6 + 6 = 9 + 6
    3x = 15
    − 6 and + 6 cancel (they make 0). 9 + 6 = 15.
  4. 3Divide both sides by 3.
    3x3 = 153
    x = 5
    3 ÷ 3 = 1, so only x is left. 15 ÷ 3 = 5.
Check: 7(5) − 6 = 29 and 4(5) + 9 = 29 ✓
4Solve. 5(x − 2) + 3x = 2(x + 7)
Solution
  1. Start
    5(x − 2) + 3x = 2(x + 7)
  2. 1Distribute on both sides.
    5 · x − 5 · 2 + 3x = 2 · x + 2 · 7
    5x − 10 + 3x = 2x + 14
    5(x − 2) = 5x − 10 and 2(x + 7) = 2x + 14.
  3. 2Combine like terms on the left.
    5x − 10 + 3x = 2x + 14
    8x − 10 = 2x + 14
    The blue x-terms are like terms: 5x + 3x = 8x.
  4. 3Subtract 2x from both sides.
    8x − 2x − 10 = 2x − 2x + 14
    6x − 10 = 14
    2x − 2x cancel on the right. On the left, 8x − 2x = 6x.
  5. 4Add 10 to both sides.
    6x − 10 + 10 = 14 + 10
    6x = 24
    − 10 and + 10 cancel (they make 0). 14 + 10 = 24.
  6. 5Divide both sides by 6.
    6x6 = 246
    x = 4
    6 ÷ 6 = 1, so only x is left. 24 ÷ 6 = 4.
Check: 5(4 − 2) + 3(4) = 22 and 2(4 + 7) = 22 ✓
Part 2 · NC.8.EE.7

One, None, or Infinitely Many Solutions

Simplify both sides. Then move the x-terms to one side. Watch what happens to the x-terms:

  • One solution: the x-terms do not all cancel. You get x = a number (even x = 0).
  • No solution: the x-terms cancel and what is left is false, like 6 = 1.
  • Infinitely many: the x-terms cancel and what is left is true, like 8 = 8.

Step through all three samples. Look for the red lines where the x-terms cancel.

Sample 1 · One solution: 5x − 3 = 2x + 9
Sample 2 · No solution: 3(x + 2) = 3x + 1
Sample 3 · Infinitely many: 4x + 8 − x = 3(x + 2) + 2
5How many solutions? 6x + 3 = 6x + 3
Solution
  1. Start
    6x + 3 = 6x + 3
  2. 1Subtract 6x from both sides.
    6x − 6x + 3 = 6x − 6x + 3
    3 = 3
    6x − 6x = 0 on BOTH sides. The x-terms are gone.
  3. 2Read what is left.
    3 = 3 is always TRUE → Infinitely many solutions
    The x-terms cancelled and what is left is TRUE. Every value of x works.
Check: Try x = 10: 6(10) + 3 = 63 on both sides ✓
6How many solutions? 4x − 1 = 4x + 5
Solution
  1. Start
    4x − 1 = 4x + 5
  2. 1Subtract 4x from both sides.
    4x − 4x − 1 = 4x − 4x + 5
    −1 = 5
    4x − 4x = 0 on BOTH sides. The x-terms are gone.
  3. 2Read what is left.
    −1 = 5 is never true → No solution
    The x-terms cancelled and what is left is FALSE. No value of x works.
7How many solutions? 2(x + 5) = 3x + 10
Solution
  1. Start
    2(x + 5) = 3x + 10
  2. 1Distribute: multiply the 2 by EACH term inside.
    2 · x + 2 · 5 = 3x + 10
    2x + 10 = 3x + 10
  3. 2Subtract 2x from both sides.
    2x − 2x + 10 = 3x − 2x + 10
    10 = x + 10
    2x − 2x cancel on the left. On the right, 3x − 2x = x. There is still an x, so keep solving.
  4. 3Subtract 10 from both sides.
    10 − 10 = x + 10 − 10
    0 = x
    + 10 and − 10 cancel on the right. 10 − 10 = 0 on the left.
  5. 4Read the answer.
    x = 0 → One solution
    The x-terms did NOT all cancel. x = 0 is a real answer, so there is ONE solution.
Check: 2(0 + 5) = 10 and 3(0) + 10 = 10 ✓
8How many solutions? 3(2x − 1) + 4 = 6x + 1
Solution
  1. Start
    3(2x − 1) + 4 = 6x + 1
  2. 1Distribute: multiply the 3 by EACH term inside.
    3 · 2x − 3 · 1 + 4 = 6x + 1
    6x − 3 + 4 = 6x + 1
  3. 2Combine the numbers on the left.
    6x − 3 + 4 = 6x + 1
    6x + 1 = 6x + 1
    The purple numbers: −3 + 4 = 1.
  4. 3Subtract 6x from both sides.
    6x − 6x + 1 = 6x − 6x + 1
    1 = 1
    6x − 6x = 0 on BOTH sides.
  5. 4Read what is left.
    1 = 1 is always TRUE → Infinitely many solutions
    The x-terms cancelled and what is left is TRUE. Every value of x works.
Check: Try x = 2: 3(3) + 4 = 13 and 6(2) + 1 = 13 ✓
Part 3 · NC.8.EE.7

Solving and Graphing Inequalities

Solve an inequality like an equation. The flip rule: when you multiply or divide by a negative, flip the sign. Then graph it: < and > use an open circle. ≤ and ≥ use a closed circle. Shade toward the numbers that work. Type answers like x > 4 or x <= -2.

Example: −2x + 1 ≥ 9
9Solve. Then graph your answer. x − 4 > 3
Solution
  1. Start
    x − 4 > 3
  2. 1Add 4 to both sides.
    x − 4 + 4 > 3 + 4
    x > 7
    − 4 and + 4 cancel (they make 0). 3 + 4 = 7. Adding does not flip the sign.
Check: Test x = 8: 8 − 4 = 4, and 4 > 3 ✓
10Solve. Then graph your answer. 3x + 2 ≤ 14
Solution
  1. Start
    3x + 2 ≤ 14
  2. 1Subtract 2 from both sides.
    3x + 2 − 2 ≤ 14 − 2
    3x ≤ 12
    + 2 and − 2 cancel. 14 − 2 = 12.
  3. 2Divide both sides by 3.
    3x3 ≤ 123
    x ≤ 4
    3 ÷ 3 = 1, so only x is left. 12 ÷ 3 = 4. The sign stays the same.
Check: Test x = 0: 3(0) + 2 = 2, and 2 ≤ 14 ✓
11Solve. Then graph your answer. −5x < 30
Solution
  1. Start
    −5x < 30
  2. 1Divide both sides by −5. Dividing by a negative flips the sign.
    −5x−5 > 30−5
    x > −6
    −5 ÷ (−5) = 1, so only x is left. < flips to > because −5 is negative.
Check: Test x = 0: −5(0) = 0, and 0 < 30 ✓
12Solve. Then graph your answer. 2(x − 3) ≥ 5x + 9
Solution
  1. Start
    2(x − 3) ≥ 5x + 9
  2. 1Distribute: multiply the 2 by EACH term inside.
    2 · x − 2 · 3 ≥ 5x + 9
    2x − 6 ≥ 5x + 9
    2 · x = 2x and 2 · 3 = 6.
  3. 2Subtract 5x from both sides.
    2x − 5x − 6 ≥ 5x − 5x + 9
    −3x − 6 ≥ 9
    5x − 5x cancel on the right. On the left, 2x − 5x = −3x.
  4. 3Add 6 to both sides.
    −3x − 6 + 6 ≥ 9 + 6
    −3x ≥ 15
    − 6 and + 6 cancel (they make 0). 9 + 6 = 15.
  5. 4Divide both sides by −3. Dividing by a negative flips the sign.
    −3x−3 ≤ 15−3
    x ≤ −5
    −3 ÷ (−3) = 1, so only x is left. ≥ flips to ≤ because −3 is negative.
Check: Test x = −6: 2(−9) = −18 and 5(−6) + 9 = −21. −18 ≥ −21 ✓
13Graph x < 2 on the number line.
Solution
  1. Given
    x < 2
  2. 1Read the sign.
    < means "less than." 2 itself is NOT included.
  3. 2Choose the circle.
    Not included → OPEN circle at 2.
  4. 3Choose the direction.
    Less than → smaller numbers → shade LEFT.
Open circle at 2, shaded left.

Parallel Lines Cut by a Transversal

Equal: corresponding, alternate interior, alternate exterior, vertical. Add to 180°: same-side interior, same-side exterior, linear pair. Interior = between the parallel lines. Alternate = opposite sides of the transversal.

Example: m∠2 = 70°. Find m∠7.
14Use the diagram. What kind of pair are ∠3 and ∠6?
Solution
  1. Given
    ∠3 and ∠6
  2. 1Where are they?
    Both are BETWEEN lines m and n → interior.
  3. 2Which side of t?
    ∠3 is left of t. ∠6 is right of t → opposite sides → alternate.
Alternate Interior angles. They are EQUAL.
15Use the diagram. What kind of pair are ∠1 and ∠7?
Solution
  1. Given
    ∠1 and ∠7
  2. 1Where are they?
    ∠1 is above line m. ∠7 is below line n. Both are OUTSIDE the parallel lines → exterior.
  3. 2Which side of t?
    Both are left of t → same side.
Same-Side Exterior angles. They ADD to 180°.
16Lines are parallel. Find x.
Solution
  1. Given
    Parallel lines are cut by a transversal. The angles are 118° and x°.
  2. 1They are in the same position at each crossing. Corresponding angles are EQUAL.
    x = 118
    Equal pair, so x is the same as the given angle.
Check: Corresponding angles are equal: 118° = 118° ✓
17Lines are parallel. Find x.
Solution
  1. Given
    Parallel lines are cut by a transversal. The angles are 64° and x°.
  2. 1Both are between the lines, on the same side of t. Same-side interior angles ADD to 180°.
    64 + x = 180
  3. 2Subtract 64 from both sides.
    64 − 64 + x = 180 − 64
    x = 116
    64 − 64 = 0. 180 − 64 = 116.
Check: 64 + 116 = 180 ✓
18Lines are parallel. The alternate interior angles are (3x + 15)° and 75°. Solve for x.
Solution
  1. Given
    Parallel lines are cut by a transversal. The angles are (3x + 15)° and 75°.
  2. 1Both are between the lines, on opposite sides of t. Alternate interior angles are EQUAL.
    3x + 15 = 75
  3. 2Subtract 15 from both sides.
    3x + 15 − 15 = 75 − 15
    3x = 60
    + 15 and − 15 cancel (they make 0). 75 − 15 = 60.
  4. 3Divide both sides by 3.
    3x3 = 603
    x = 20
    3 ÷ 3 = 1, so only x is left. 60 ÷ 3 = 20.
Check: 3(20) + 15 = 75 ✓
Part 5 · NC.8.G.5

Triangle Interior and Exterior Angles

Triangle Sum Theorem: the three interior angles add to 180°. Exterior Angle Theorem: an exterior angle equals the sum of the two remote interior angles (the two inside angles that do not touch it).

Example: A triangle has angles 40° and 65°. Find the exterior angle next to the third angle.
19Find x.
Solution
  1. Given
    A triangle has angles 52°, 79° and x°.
  2. 1All three angles are inside the triangle. They add to 180°.
    52 + 79 + x = 180
  3. 2Combine the numbers on the left.
    52 + 79 + x = 180
    131 + x = 180
    The purple numbers: 52 + 79 = 131.
  4. 3Subtract 131 from both sides.
    131 − 131 + x = 180 − 131
    x = 49
    131 − 131 = 0. 180 − 131 = 49.
Check: 52 + 79 + 49 = 180 ✓
20Find the exterior angle x.
Solution
  1. Given
    The remote interior angles are 38° and 77°. The exterior angle is x°.
  2. 1x is an exterior angle. It equals the sum of the two remote interior angles.
    x = 38 + 77
  3. 2Add the two remote angles.
    x = 38 + 77
    x = 115
Check: The inside angle at that corner is 65°, and 65 + 115 = 180 ✓
21Find x.
Solution
  1. Given
    The exterior angle is 130°. One remote interior angle is 72°. The other is x°.
  2. 1The exterior angle equals the sum of the two remote interior angles.
    130 = 72 + x
  3. 2Subtract 72 from both sides.
    130 − 72 = 72 − 72 + x
    58 = x
    72 − 72 = 0. 130 − 72 = 58. So x = 58.
Check: 72 + 58 = 130 ✓
22The angles of a triangle are (x + 5)°, (2x + 10)°, and (3x + 15)°. Solve for x.
Solution
  1. Given
    The angles of a triangle are (x + 5)°, (2x + 10)° and (3x + 15)°.
  2. 1The three interior angles add to 180°.
    (x + 5) + (2x + 10) + (3x + 15) = 180
  3. 2Combine like terms: blue x-terms together, purple numbers together.
    x + 5 + 2x + 10 + 3x + 15 = 180
    6x + 30 = 180
    x + 2x + 3x = 6x. 5 + 10 + 15 = 30.
  4. 3Subtract 30 from both sides.
    6x + 30 − 30 = 180 − 30
    6x = 150
    + 30 and − 30 cancel (they make 0). 180 − 30 = 150.
  5. 4Divide both sides by 6.
    6x6 = 1506
    x = 25
    6 ÷ 6 = 1, so only x is left. 150 ÷ 6 = 25.
Check: The angles are 30°, 60° and 90°. 30 + 60 + 90 = 180 ✓
