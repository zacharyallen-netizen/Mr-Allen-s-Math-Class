Units 3–4

Solving multi-step equations with variables on both sides, distributing, and equations with one, none, or infinite solutions.

What you will learn in Unit 3

Learning targets

  • I can solve multi-step equations, including ones with variables on both sides
  • I can distribute and combine like terms accurately
  • I can identify equations with one solution, no solution, or infinitely many

Standards

  • 8.EE.7Solve equations and inequalities in one variable, with one, none or infinitely many solutions.
