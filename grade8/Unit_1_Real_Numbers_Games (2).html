🎯 Estimating Square Roots — the "Warmer / Colder" Hunt

A non-perfect square root is hiding on the number line. Your only tool is squaring your guess.

🔍 How the hunt works

Square your guess and compare it to the target:
❄️ guess² too small → colder (guess too low) 🔥 guess² too big → hotter (guess too high) 🎯 closest → found it!
The algorithm: ① Trap it between the two perfect squares just below & above → gives the whole number. ② Zoom into the tenths and test by squaring — warmer or colder? ③ Land on the tenth whose square is closest (round to the nearest tenth).
