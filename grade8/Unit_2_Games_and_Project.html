📋 Numbers of Our World — Scientific Notation Project

Your project assignment and rubric. Use the tools above to research, convert, and compare your numbers.

  1. Choose 5 facts that interest you. Each must use a number greater than 1,000 or less than 0.001 (so it needs a positive or negative exponent). Try space, technology, population, environment, or microscopic science.
  2. Convert each number into scientific notation. (The converter above can check your work.)
  3. Compile your data creatively into a visual project with a cover titled "Numbers of Our World in Scientific Notation." Write each fact twice — standard form and scientific notation — with a colorful image beside it.
  4. List your references — the title and URL of each credible science/education source.

⭐ To earn a Level 5

Compare and contrast your data with other data points using phrases like "greater than" or "times greater than." These require operations with scientific notation — so show your work!

Worked comparison (from the exemplar):
Sirius A produces 2.5 × 10²⁷ W; the Sun produces 3.8 × 10²⁶ W.
(2.5 × 10²⁷) ÷ (3.8 × 10²⁶) = (2.5 ÷ 3.8) × 10¹ ≈ 0.66 × 10¹ = 6.6
→ Sirius A gives off about 6.6 times more energy than our Sun.
