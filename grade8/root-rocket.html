ROOT ROCKET

Your rocket must land on the pad at a square root. The pad is hidden on the number line. Use your estimate to land on it.

  1. Trap it: tap the two whole numbers the root is between.
  2. Aim: drag the rocket (or use ◀ ▶) to your estimate to the nearest tenth.
  3. Land! Right on the tenth = Perfect landing. One tenth off = good. Too far = crash, and you try again.

Stuck? Press Hint. The flight computer uses the 4-step estimate:
√n ≈ lower root + (n − lower²) ÷ (upper² − lower²)
