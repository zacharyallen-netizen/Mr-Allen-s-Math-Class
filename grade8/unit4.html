Units 3–4

What you will learn in Unit 4

Learning targets

  • I can use angle relationships formed by parallel lines and a transversal
  • I can apply the Triangle Angle-Sum and Exterior-Angle Theorems
  • I can solve for unknown angles by writing and solving equations

Standards

  • 8.G.5Use angle facts: parallel lines cut by a transversal, triangle angle sums and exterior angles.
