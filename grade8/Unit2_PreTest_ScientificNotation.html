SCORE3.0Basic Understanding

NC.8.EE.3 Express very large and very small quantities in scientific notation and standard form.

• I can write numbers in scientific notation and in standard form, and recognize correct form.

Match each vocabulary term to its definition, then write each number as directed.

scientific notation A. A number written the ordinary way, such as 45,000.
coefficient B. The decimal factor in a × 10ⁿ — at least 1 and less than 10.
standard form C. Shows a small number between 0 and 1 (move the decimal left).
base D. A number written as a × 10ⁿ, where 1 ≤ a < 10.
positive exponent E. In a power of ten, the 10 that is multiplied repeatedly.
negative exponent F. Shows a large number, 10 or greater (move the decimal right).
