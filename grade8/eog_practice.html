Calculator help

Using Desmos

On the Calculator OK part of the test, you get a Desmos calculator. Open it from the test’s tool bar. To practice at home, go to desmos.com/calculator.

  • Exponents: type ^. 2^5 means 25. Press the right arrow key to get out of the exponent.
  • Parentheses: use them a lot. (−3)^2 is 9, but −3^2 is −9.
  • Fractions: type / to make a fraction. Click the fraction ⇄ decimal button next to an answer to switch how it looks.
  • Square root: type sqrt. Type pi for π.
  • Solve an equation: graph each side. Type y = 2x + 5 and y = 3x + 21. Click the gray dot where the lines cross. The x-value is the answer.
  • Tables: click +, then table. Type the x and y values. If the dots make a straight line, the table is linear.
  • Slope from two points: type (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁) with real numbers, like (29 − 9)/(6 − 1).
  • Sliders: type a letter in an equation, like y = kx + 2. Click add slider for k. Drag it to see the line change.

Part 1 · NC.8.NS.1 · NC.8.NS.2

Number System

What the test asks

You sort numbers into rational and irrational. You also estimate a square root that is not a whole number. Know your perfect squares up to 152 = 225.

Part 2 · NC.8.EE.1 – NC.8.EE.8

Expressions & Equations

What the test asks

You use exponent rules and scientific notation. You find square roots of perfect squares. You solve equations with x on both sides, and you find how many solutions an equation has. Word problems ask when two costs are the same.

Part 3 · NC.8.F.1 – NC.8.F.5

Functions

What the test asks

You decide if a graph, table, or set of points is a function, and if an equation is linear. You find slope and y-intercept from tables and words. You compare two functions and read a graph that tells a story.

Part 4 · NC.8.G.3 – NC.8.G.9

Geometry

What the test asks

You move shapes on a grid with transformations. You use angle facts for parallel lines and triangles. You use the Pythagorean Theorem and find distance. You find the volume of cylinders and cones.

Part 5 · NC.8.SP.1 – NC.8.SP.4

Statistics & Probability

What the test asks

You read scatter plots and name the association. You pick the line that fits the data best. You tell what the slope and y-intercept mean in a story. You build a two-way table.
