Fractions · Module 4
Make fractions simpler, then add, subtract, multiply, and divide them, one step at a time. To add or subtract, the bottom numbers have to match. Use the lowest common denominator. To multiply, go straight across. To divide, flip the second fraction and multiply. You are only racing your own best score.
Here is one worked out
5/6 − 1/4 — find the lowest common denominator first
Count by 6s: 6, 12. 4 goes into 12 evenly, so the LCD is 12 (not 24). 6 × 2 = 12, so multiply the top by 2 too. 4 × 3 = 12, so multiply the top by 3. Then subtract the tops: 10 − 3 = 7.
