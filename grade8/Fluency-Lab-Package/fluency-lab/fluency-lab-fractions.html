Fractions · Module 4
  • To add or subtract, the bottoms must match.
  • To multiply, go straight across. To divide, flip the second fraction.
  • Only one box opens at a time. It glows.
  • You are only racing your own best score.
Here is one worked out: 5/6 − 1/4
Count by 6s: 6, 12. 4 goes into 12, so the bottoms both become 12.
6 × 2 = 12, so 5 × 2 = 10. 4 × 3 = 12, so 1 × 3 = 3.
10 − 3 = 7. The answer is 7/12.
