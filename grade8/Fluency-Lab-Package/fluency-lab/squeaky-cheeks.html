Squeaky Cheeks

You are in a crowded room. You would like it less crowded. Answer math problems to build up gas. Then walk over to a group of people and let one rip. Clear out enough people to win the room.

1. AnswerType the answer and press Enter. 5 right fills your gas meter.
2. WalkClick or tap a spot in the room to walk there.
3. TootPress the T key or the TOOT button. Get close to groups for a bigger stink.
4. UpgradeEvery right answer earns beans. Spend beans on stronger toots.

A wrong answer gives a sad little pfft and the room laughs at you. That problem comes back later.
