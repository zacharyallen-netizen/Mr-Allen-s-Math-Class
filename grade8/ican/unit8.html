G.3-9Mastery (5)

Try thisRectangle WXYZ has W(0, 0), X(4, 0), Y(4, 3), Z(0, 3). It is dilated by a scale factor of 3 about the origin. a) Give the image coordinates. b) Compare the side lengths, perimeter, and area of the two rectangles. c) Explain why a dilation makes a similar figure but not a congruent one, and why the area grows by more than 3 times.
Show answer
Answer: W′(0, 0), X′(12, 0), Y′(12, 9), Z′(0, 9); sides and perimeter ×3 (14 → 42); area ×9 (12 → 108)

  3. Area 4 × 3 = 12 → 12 × 9 = 108, which is 9 = 3² times as big.

  5. Area uses two lengths (length × width), and each is multiplied by k, so area is multiplied by k · k = k².

G.4-9Mastery (5)

I can use similar figures to solve real-world indirect measurement problems and justify my reasoning.

Try thisOn a sunny afternoon, Luis is 5 ft tall and casts a shadow 4 ft long. At the same time, a tree next to him casts a shadow 36 ft long. How tall is the tree? Justify why you can use similar triangles.
Show answer
Answer: The tree is 45 ft tall
  1. Luis and the tree both stand straight up, so each makes a 90° angle with the ground.
  2. The sun's rays hit both at the same angle at the same time.
  3. Two pairs of equal angles → the triangles are similar (AA).
  4. Set up a proportion: 5/4 = h/36.
  5. h = 36 × 5 ÷ 4 = 45 ft.
