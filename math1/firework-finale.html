Firework Finale

You are the show designer. Every firework flies on a parabola, y = a(x − h)² + k, and bursts at the very top: the vertex. Read each equation, aim the shell, and build a sky full of fireworks for the grand finale.

1. Read the equationVertex form, factored form or standard form. Each one shows you something.
2. AimTap the vertex, zeros, axis or height. A wrong aim fizzles, and you see why.
3. Grand finaleAfter 8 fireworks, every one you launched lights the sky at once.
Keys 1–4 tap answers. Every show is open.
