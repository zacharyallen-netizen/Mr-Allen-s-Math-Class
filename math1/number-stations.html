Number Stations

The city's metro is dark. Every metro line is a sequence, and every station is a term. Solve the pattern to power up the next station and keep your train moving. Light up all five lines to bring the city back to life.

1. Read the boardThe departure board shows the terms. The ? is the station you need.
2. Tap your answerA wrong answer tells you the mistake. You get 3 tries and a Hint.
3. Grow your trainEach first-try answer adds a train car and bonus points.
Keys 1–4 tap answers. Nothing is locked: ride any line.
