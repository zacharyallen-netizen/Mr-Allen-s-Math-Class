Part B · The Distance Formula

Type the distance as a number, like 5 or sqrt(13). d = √(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²

B Finding the Distance

1 Find the distance between the two points shown, (1, 1) and (4, 5).
d = √[(4−1)² + (5−1)²] = √[9 + 16] = √25 → d = 5 (a 3-4-5 right triangle)
2 Find the distance between (0, 0) and (6, 8).
d = √[(6−0)² + (8−0)²] = √[36 + 64] = √100 → d = 10
3 Find the distance between (−2, 3) and (2, 0).
d = √[(2−(−2))² + (0−3)²] = √[16 + 9] = √25 → d = 5
4 Find the distance between (1, −2) and (4, 4). Give the exact answer, like sqrt(45), or round to 2 decimals.
Δx = 3, Δy = 4 − (−2) = 6. d = √(9 + 36) = √45 ≈ 6.71

  1. Δx = 5 − (−1) = 6, Δy = 7 − (−1) = 8
  2. 6² + 8² = 36 + 64 = 100
  3. d = 100
5 Which step has the mistake?
d = √100 = 10

Part D · Classifying Polygons on the Coordinate Plane

Use the figure ABCD to answer Questions 1–5. Each question has its own copy of the grid.

D Using Slope & Distance to Classify

1 Using slope, what type of quadrilateral is ABCD?
All four angles are right angles
2 Find the length of side AB.
A(0,0) to B(4,0): d = √[(4−0)² + (0−0)²] = √16 → AB = 4
3 Find the length of side BC.
B(4,0) to C(4,3): d = √[(4−4)² + (3−0)²] = √9 → BC = 3
4 Find the length of the diagonal AC.
A(0,0) to C(4,3): d = √[(4−0)² + (3−0)²] = √[16+9] = √25 → AC = 5
5 Explain how the distance formula shows that ABCD is NOT a rhombus (a rhombus has 4 equal sides).
AB = 4 and BC = 3.
Since 4 ≠ 3, not all sides are equal, so ABCD is not a rhombus — it's a rectangle (only opposite sides equal, all right angles).

Part E · Perimeter & Area on the Plane

Perimeter = add all the sides. Area of a rectangle = length × width. Area of a triangle = 12 × base × height.

E Perimeter & Area from Coordinates

1 Find the perimeter of triangle ABC with A(1, 1), B(5, 1), C(5, 4).
AB = 4, BC = 3, AC = √(16 + 9) = 5. Perimeter = 4 + 3 + 5 = 12 units
2 Find the area of triangle ABC.
½ × 4 × 3 = 6 square units
3 Find the area of rectangle PQRS with P(−3, −2), Q(2, −2), R(2, 4), S(−3, 4).
