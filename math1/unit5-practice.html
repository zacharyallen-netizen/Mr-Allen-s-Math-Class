Part A · Reading a Histogram

Use the histogram of test scores to answer Questions 1–4.

A Frequency & Distribution

1 How many students scored in the 80–89 range?
✓ The 80–89 bar reaches a height of 10.
2 How many total students are represented in the histogram?
Add every bar's height: 3 + 7 + 10 + 4 → 24 students
3 Which interval is the mode (the range with the most students)?
The tallest bar is 80–89 with 10 students → 80–89
4 What percent of students scored below 80?
Below 80 = 3 + 7 = 10 students out of 24 total. 10 ÷ 24 ≈ 0.417 → ≈ 41.7%

Part B · Reading a Box Plot

Use the box plot (five-number summary) to answer Questions 1–4.

B Median, IQR & Outliers

1 What is the median shown in the box plot?
The line inside the box marks the median → 7
2 What is the interquartile range (IQR = Q3 − Q1)?
IQR = Q3 − Q1 = 9 − 5 → IQR = 4
3 What is the range of the entire data set (max − min)?
Range = max − min = 14 − 2 → 12
4 Using the 1.5×IQR rule, is the maximum value (14) an outlier? Show your work, then reveal.
IQR = 9 − 5 = 4.
Upper fence = Q3 + 1.5(IQR) = 9 + 1.5(4) = 9 + 6 = 15.
Since 14 < 15, 14 is NOT an outlier.

Part D · Residuals & Correlation vs. Causation

Click Reveal to check your reasoning.

D Interpreting the Model

RESIDUAL A student studied 4 hours and scored 82. The trend line predicts 6(4) + 60 = 84. What is the residual (actual − predicted)?
Residual = actual − predicted = 82 − 84 = −2.
CAUSATION A study finds a strong positive correlation between ice cream sales and drowning incidents. Does this mean ice cream sales cause drownings? Explain.
No — correlation does not imply causation. A lurking variable, warm weather, likely increases both ice cream sales and swimming (and therefore drownings).
RESIDUAL A different student studied 2 hours and scored 75. The trend line predicts 6(2)+60=72. Find the residual, and state whether the trend line under- or over-predicted.
Residual = 75 − 72 = +3. Since the residual is positive, the trend line under-predicted this student's score.
