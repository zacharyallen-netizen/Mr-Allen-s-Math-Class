Drone Intercept

Two runaway delivery drones are flying straight paths over the city. Each path is a line, and each line has an equation. Find where the two paths cross (the solution of the system) and fire your net to catch both drones at once.

1. Read the signalsEach drone sends its path as an equation. Drone 1 is blue, drone 2 is pink.
2. Solve the systemGraph, substitute or eliminate. Tap the point where they cross.
3. Fire the netRight point: both drones caught. Wrong point: the net misses and tells you why.
Keys 1–4 tap answers. Every sector is open.
