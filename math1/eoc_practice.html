Using Desmos

On the EOC, questions marked Calculator OK let you use Desmos. Questions marked No calculator are done by hand.

Open Desmos in a new tab

The calculator is open in the panel at the bottom of the screen.

  1. Open it. Go to desmos.com/calculator, or use the Desmos button inside the test. You can also tap the green Desmos button at the bottom of this page.
  2. Use parentheses. Type 3(2x-5) and 20(0.8)^x. Press the right arrow to get out of an exponent.
  3. Solve an equation. Graph each side as its own line: y=3x+5 and y=-2x-10. Click where they cross. The x-value of that point is the answer.
  4. Zeros and vertex. Graph the function. Click the gray dots on the graph. Dots on the x-axis are the zeros. The top or bottom dot is the vertex.
  5. Tables. Click + then table. Type the data in the x1 and y1 columns.
  6. Regression. Under the table, type y1~mx1+b for a line of best fit. Type y1~a·b^x1 for an exponential model. Read r (the correlation coefficient), m, b, and a in the box below it.
  7. Use the model. After a regression, type 14m+b to predict y when x = 14. Type e1 to see the residuals.
  8. Sliders. Type y=mx+b and click “add slider” to see what m and b do.
  9. Fraction toggle. Type a number like 0.8. Click the small fraction button next to the answer to see it as a fraction.

Section 1 · N-RN.2

Number & Quantity

These questions use the exponent rules. For a power of a power, multiply the exponents. When you divide like bases, subtract the exponents.

Section 2 · A-SSE · A-APR · A-CED · A-REI

Algebra: Equations, Inequalities & Polynomials

Write and solve equations, inequalities, and systems. Multiply and factor polynomials. In word problems, say what each variable stands for before you write anything.

Section 3 · F-IF · F-BF · F-LE

Functions: Linear, Exponential & Quadratic

Linear functions add the same amount each step. Exponential functions multiply by the same amount each step. Quadratic functions make a parabola with a highest or lowest point.

Section 4 · S-ID

Statistics

Describe data with its center and spread, and watch for outliers. Use Desmos to find the line of best fit, the correlation coefficient r, and the residuals.

Section 5 · G-GPE

Geometry: Coordinate Geometry

Use slope, midpoint, and distance on the coordinate plane. Parallel lines have the same slope. Perpendicular lines have slopes that are opposite reciprocals.
