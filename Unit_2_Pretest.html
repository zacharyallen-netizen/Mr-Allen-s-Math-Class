SCORE3.0Basic Understanding

NC.8.EE.1 Develop and apply the properties of integer exponents to generate equivalent numerical expressions.

• I can apply the properties of integer exponents to simplify expressions with numerical bases.

Match each vocabulary term to its definition, then simplify each expression.

base A. The numerical factor multiplied by the variable(s) in a term.
exponent B. Tells how many times the base is used as a factor.
coefficient C. Terms with identical variable parts (same variables to the same powers).
monomial D. A number or variable used as a repeated factor in a power.
like terms E. An expression that uses a root symbol, such as a square root (√).
radical F. A single term: a number, a variable, or a product of numbers and variables.
