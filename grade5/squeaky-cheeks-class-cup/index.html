Squeaky Cheeks

You are in a crowded room. You would like it less crowded. Answer math problems to build up gas. Then walk over to a group of people and let one rip. Clear out enough people to win the room.

1. AnswerUse the picture to help. Type the answer and press Enter. 5 right fills your gas meter.
2. WalkClick or tap a spot in the room to walk there.
3. TootPress the T key or the TOOT button. Get close to groups for a bigger stink.
4. UpgradeEvery right answer earns beans. Spend beans on stronger toots.

A wrong answer gives a sad little pfft, tells you what went wrong, and that problem comes back later. Fractions can be typed as a fraction (like 7/4) or a mixed number (like 1 3/4). Any equal fraction counts.

Pick any room. Rooms 1–2: volume (NC.5.MD.4–5). Rooms 3–4: multiplying fractions and area (NC.5.NF.4). Room 5: dividing with unit fractions (NC.5.NF.7). Room 6: review.

Which homeroom are you in?

Every right answer earns 1 point for your homeroom. Clearing a room earns 10 bonus points. Only class totals are kept. Your name is never saved.
